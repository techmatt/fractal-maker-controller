## What binds a gallery
- **★ THE POOL HOLDS n.** At n=1000 and n=2000 an integer program over the view fills the gallery with every linear rule; one-per-location, group cap and family allowance refuse nothing — **but spiral DOES refuse and binds exactly**, 10.1% of seats in every run since `DEFAULT_SPIRAL_CAP` shipped 2026-09-04 (measured ckpt 114 across four seatings; → `curation/MEASUREMENTS.md`). **The greedy seed's shortfall was ORDERING** — it overspends colour and 1-swap conserves seat count, so tier 1 was frozen at the seed; "the allowance binds at every n" was a fact about the greedy's path, not the pool. The augmenting chain (ckpt 106) fills n≤1000 exhaustively; **n=2000 filled UNAIDED on the 2026-09-18 pool (seed 2,000, chain stage found nothing, 148.8 s)** — the old "n=2000 is a chain-budget question" reading was a fact about the 2026-09-04 pool, not about `n` (→ `GALLERY.md §The augmenting chain`).
- **★ THE TWIN RULE: τ at 0.90× of the refit since ckpt 107 (Matt's eye on a boundary sheet), GLOBAL, one number.** The colour-AND-geometry gate is DEAD; two near-white pictures with different geometry collapse in a colour signature. **At n=1000 τ moves NO seat count** (every candidate fills), only worst/sum and which thousand. ⚠ **The twin rule holds back about a third of the view** — the rejection ledger's twin count undercounts by rule order (twin runs last); read the crossing sweep, never the ledger. **A themed pass swaps the twin test for the geometry-only `Places` rule — τ is not in the themed path.** Evidence → `GALLERY.md §The twin metric`.
- **★ AT n=2000 THE CONSTRAINTS CHOOSE THE GALLERY, NOT THE JUDGE.** The judge has no order inside its top (→ fractal-corpus); a judge flip turned over three quarters of the seats and Matt read both as fine. **Retrains buy FLOOR; the top end is Matt's eye on the final seating.** At n=2000 the binding rule is `location`/`twin`, not colour (2026-09-18).
- **★ A MODE FLOOR IS A MANDATE, AND THE MANDATE IS A BETTER CHAIN SEED THAN THE RANKED WALK (2026-09-18).** So the floor is NOT monotone: lowering it to clear a tier-2 shortfall COSTS tier-1 seats (magenta n=400: floor 0 → 396 seats, flat 4 → 399, `seat_floors(n)` → 400). A rising shortfall under a fuller gallery is the lexicographic objective working, not a regression. Every collection pass floors at `mode_policy.seat_floors(n)` since 2026-09-18 (→ fractal-state, `GALLERY.md`). A mode pass's residual shortfall is colour `cell_floor` demand, not mode floors.
- **★ FOR A MODE COLLECTION, PLACES A SIBLING MODE ALREADY SEATS BUY MORE THAN THE GRADED STOCK** — 37.3 vs 23.5 seats per engine-hour at the ceilings on 2026-09-18, and the graded list weakens down its own order (11.3% clears on the top 150 places, 5.8% below). The smooth composites share no field dump: every candidate is a full render. Texture weight, drawn 0.3–0.9: the judges' clear rate falls monotonically with weight (~2× low third to high) and seats still come from every third — judge-only until Matt reads the seats (→ fractal-state §MODES AND COLOUR).
- **★ A NEW MAP'S SEAT USUALLY TAKES A PLACE THE GALLERY WAS NOT USING, NOT ANOTHER MAP'S SEAT (ckpt 109).** Of 54 new-map seats in a fresh n=1000, **33 sat at places the baseline gallery did not hold, 17 were already seats, and 4 took the place off another row**; across seven records there were five same-place swaps in total, and distinct maps seated ROSE 528 → 542. An enlarged pool RE-SEATS rather than substitutes. **Never model new supply's effect as displacement.**
- **★ SET CONSTRAINTS COUNTED AGAINST SEATS FILLED BIND DURING THE WALK** (the spiral cap since ckpt 106; the `threads` ceiling since ckpt 108 (value → fractal-tutorial §Selection) — refusals early in the walk and a better sum at a final count under the cap). ## Mining economics — laws only (every rate → `curation/MEASUREMENTS.md`)

- **★ RARE-CELL AIMING (`--draw-cells`, the aimed arm) IS AN n≤1000 COMPOSITION INSTRUMENT — RULED ckpt 106, not a mining policy.** Its lift, its quality cost and the saturation refusal → fractal-state §SOURCING and `curation/LEGS.md`. A thin cell is STOCK-bound, not carrier-bound (→ fractal-state, `palettes/README.md`).
- **★ THE NEAR BAND IS A FIRST PASS AT A PLACE, CONSUMABLE, AND A MODE'S CLEAR RATE IS TWO NUMBERS, OPENER AND DEEPENER.** Its ceiling is the embedding store, 88,181 vectors after the ckpt-126 admissions; a place with no vector is invisible to every `hunt.scanned` arm, and `curate score --opened` / `--graded` are the doors (fractal-state §SOURCING). Sized with room under `RETAIN_PER_PAIR` it nets one row per free slot and the rest of its value is quality (`LEGS.md §--near-places`). Its pairing of a place with the incumbent's mode is the arm's CODE PATH, not a law of mining (Matt, 2026-09-14) — an ordinary depth plan with a place list renders whatever modes it names. **The durable hazard is a plan-time roster mismatch**: a manifest cut over one roster while the planner admits another plans zero units, silently, because the plan is fixed before the first render. ⚠ **A KEEP INCREASE CREATES A ONE-TIME STOCK OF FREE SLOTS THAT ONE LEG CAN CONSUME IN HOURS** — that is not a refill rate.
- **★ RECOLOURING PROVEN PLACES IS THE CHEAPEST q4** — the recolour arm and the near band are the same buy; prices → `curation/MEASUREMENTS.md`. One-per-location means it buys quality INSIDE a collection, never a new place, and there are no new places to buy (→ fractal-state §SOURCING). **★ CONDITIONING MOVES THE COLOUR COLUMN; READ A THEMED LEG AT `P(≥3) ≥ 0.50`**; it is the MAP SET, not the colour; keep drawing the unconditioned control. ⚠ **The old "~1.8× rate cut" and "its wall is CONVERSION" readings are SUPERSEDED (ckpt 125)** — against a matched same-leg control the aim cost no quality at the family read (→ fractal-state, `LEGS.md §--draw-cells`). **★ THE THIN-THEME GOAL IS A THEMED-PASS GOAL, NEVER A GENERAL-GALLERY ONE (Matt, ckpt 111)**; the colour floor still guarantees every cell one fair share in the general gallery and stays OFF on the themed path. **A colour retention KEY stays CLOSED** (→ `preserve\retention_design.md`); the one colour term the prune carries is the family ALLOWANCE (→ fractal-state §RETENTION). The colour-blind rank's cost sits BELOW the bar: of 4,981 rows one recolour leg pruned, 70 were above `Q4_BAR`, with no hue pattern. ⚠ Direct traps cannot be aimed by palette (`NOISY_MODES`) — give them unaimed time. ⚠ **`curvature` is UNMINED since ckpt 109** — still seated, still remodable, drawn by no leg (standing → fractal-tutorial §Mode policy). **`--draw-maps` aiming never consults the palette head**, so aiming a leg at named maps needs no model work. `direct_trap_screen` is the cheapest q4 mode on the roster and `direct_trap_lines` among the dearest — the direct traps are not one thing. A cell exclusion narrows the OFFER, not the outcome.
- **★ THE COLORING KIND IS A PRICE, NOT A ROSTER** — a field mode dumps once and recolours; composites, direct traps AND the angle modes render in full; **read `colorize.shareable`, never memory.** **★ ⚠ AND THE MULTIPLE IS 12–45× ON CLEARS, MEASURED (2026-09-14)** — one night's `smooth` unit took 7.1% of the engine seconds and returned 57.6% of the clears, at 6.1 engine seconds a clear against 71.6–276.4 for the dear roster, on a clear rate only 1.5× better. **Shareability, not mode quality, is the dominant cost term in a mining night**, and excluding a shareable mode on seat grounds is paid for in engine seconds. - **★ A LEG'S WIDTH IS SIZED TO FREE SLOTS, NOT TO PLACES (ckpt 109).** A leg taking free slots runs at **width 1 and keeps every row**, where width over already-full pairs merges most of what it renders. Every count in this reading is keep-dependent and retires with K → `preserve\retention_design.md`, `curation/MEASUREMENTS.md`. **A row at a PROVEN place is worth roughly three of a row at a free slot: free slots buy PRESENCE, proven places buy SEATS.**
