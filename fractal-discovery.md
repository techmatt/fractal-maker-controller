## Sourcing truths (domain knowledge)
- **★★★ MINIBROTS ARE A REFRAMING OPERATOR, NOT A SOURCE** — the operators INHERIT quality from what triggers them. Every first-principles enumerator is dead; keep a cheap periodic probe as an AUDIT — a self-filtering loop cannot see its own blind spot. **Near-minibrot [human n=360]:** beats unscreened supply 4×, beaten at the top by ranked harvest; a single rung buys ~1 look per atom, not 3 (cross-rung looks land inside the near-dup cut).
- **★★ Unscreened native-multibrot supply = ZERO** [0/144 at ≥2, ∂M-shell draw] — native mb3/4/5 run on seeds + triggered reframing ONLY; no raw draws.
- **★★ FRAMING:** 1× is a black tile for a true nucleus; 4× = triage + per-nucleus measuring frame; 16× = labelable; ladder aimed OUT. **DEGREE: never measured free of period/composition** — a degree decision needs a draw matched on both.
- **★★ MOTIF SATURATION** — julia similarity decays SMOOTHLY with |Δc|, NO knee (earlier knee figures were viewport-confounded); z-plane supply scales with DISTINCT c's — sample to the knee, then refill. The wallpapers twin channel inherits this: it is FLOOR-LIMITED by c-spacing, not closed, and run2 proved it a BATTERY (consumes primed parents ~10× faster than it makes new ones) — refill is the standing supply lever. **Twin top-sliced machine admissions are REAL** [human n=90, ckpt-55]: ≥3 = 1.000 in all three partitions, 4 ≈ .53–.60; the 0.2 machine discount understates realized value ~3× — one sitting cut labels-only deficits ~51/38/31 → 34/19/15 (mb3/4/5). The slice is a SATURATED instrument (cannot be re-asked); future twin labels draw DEEPER into the pools, and only when a decision needs them. The bound: top ~2% of each pool with flat within-slice ordering — pool TOPS proven real, pool BODIES not.
- **Family character [post-strat human]:** julia 10–33% good · mandelbrot ~14% · native mb ~12–14% · phoenix ~0.15/seed (neutral-stability skeleton; fertility structural per-seed; motif scarcity, not volume). q4 on julia ~2% structural, two-channel AND: boundary filaments + composed interior LAKES.
- **★★ MANDELBROT (c-plane) SMOOTH SUPPLY IS SEEDED, NOT DISCOVERED** [measured 2026-08-18, 94.9-min dedicated leg; record `artifacts/mandelbrot_sourcing/`]: descending beside human q3+ labels yields an offer with **53.0%±6.9 clearing the smooth advisory, a 2,640-row standing offer** (whole prior history: 1/69) — while FRESH virgin roots reproduce the historic near-zero exactly (clear-lineage 6.2% vs seeded 54.2%): the old harvest was not unlucky, and **the channel grows only as the q3+ label store grows** (754/850 seeds still undrawn — headroom before any new labels). `snap_to_nucleus` is the leg's best operator (median 0.818 vs ordinary descent 0.408) — the outward framing ladder doing its job. **Black-interior/frame-filling scarcity is ABSENT in seeded stock** (interior 0.0000 on both sides of the smooth verdict; occupancy runs backwards; nothing structural separates clears from fails) — untested hypothesis, raise-only: remaining rejects may be palette verdicts (one drawn map per location).
