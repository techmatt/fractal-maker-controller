## Mining economics — laws only (every rate → `curation/README.md`)
- **★ EVERY "PRIMED AT k" COUNT IS A MAXIMUM OVER k NOISY SCORES AND IS OPTIMISTIC.** Quote a prime count as RAW or CALIBRATED, never mixed.
- **★ PALETTES ARE EXCHANGEABLE WITHIN THE UNRESTRICTED POOL** to at least k=40 — the palette head's rank buys nothing at the boundary and k is bounded by cost alone. ⚠ **A CARRIER-RESTRICTED SUBSET IS NOT EXCHANGEABLE WITH IT.** **High k strictly dominates for seat-hunting and is exactly wrong when a one-per-location sheet is the product.**
- **★ CONDITIONING MOVES THE COLOUR COLUMN AND COSTS THE QUALITY ONE — GRADE-DEPENDENT (ckpt 96).** A conditioned lime arm hit its cell 44.8% against the control's 0.78% (57.6× lift, 22 of 24 carriers), cleared NOTHING at the q4 crossing, and was the CHEAPEST arm in the leg per q3 location at the acting bar. **"CLEARED" (a `P(≥4) ≥ 0.50` crossing) is the WRONG READOUT for a themed leg — read it at `P(≥3) ≥ 0.50`.** Conditioning is mode-dependent by design: it delivers where the COLORMAP makes the colour, not where a TRAP does.
- **★ IT IS THE MAP SET, NOT THE COLOUR.** Carriers are worse maps whatever colour they land on; a themed collection is bought by paying for a much worse score distribution and taking its thin top — draw biased, render, read the dominance the row already carries, keep what came out dominant; the carrier table is a prior, not a gate. **And a themed POOL is a near-duplicate pool under the colour-cloud twin metric** — the wall is the diversity rule, never a hunt (→ fractal-tutorial §The colour ceiling, solver_design §Themed).
- **⚠ THE LEDGER-WIDE CLEAR RATE IS NOT A DRAW RATE** — a fresh breadth draw on never-opened locations is what a new arm is read against. **⚠ Rates are ROSTER FIGURES, not constants.**
- **★ DEPTH SUBSTITUTES FOR RANK BUT DOES NOT REPLACE IT.** Draw the top half, not the whole range. **CHOOSING THE MODE ROSTER IS WORTH MORE THAN RANK.**
- **★ A BEST-AVAILABLE PERCENTILE IS NOT A MINING READOUT.** **Only `best_rank` separates a real move from the denominator.**
- **★ MINING BUYS SEATS, NOT CEILINGS.** At n=150 the tail is what binds, so that is the right purchase — where a tail exists to buy; where the cap binds, nothing does.
- **★ THE NEAR BAND IS ~20× BREADTH IN SEATS PER WALL HOUR** but cannot be aimed at a mode [ENFORCED: `depth.plan_held_mode`] and is CONSUMABLE. ⚠ **`--band-weights` with the lower bands at 0 is the flag that cuts the ranked draw, not `--top-bands`.**
- **MERGE BETWEEN LEGS, NEVER AFTER BOTH.** `curate depth` runs on the locked three workers **partitioned by LOCATION**. **The reframe channel is OPERATOR-BOUND (92% of the leg is operators; `expand_neighborhood` is 99.6% of the operator clock at ~2.6 s and 1,300k Newton solves per 6k locations, and 91% of the locations), so the render pool does not apply to it** — one `engine.screen` at a time, below normal; a crashed `engine.screen` batch is retried once then given up on. Its probe and found caps are the one untuned lever.
- **★ "CLEARED" SAYS NOTHING ABOUT HUMAN q3s.** A mode's q3 supply is UNMEASURED by that readout and a mode is never called a failure on a judge verdict alone (Matt). Novelty is worth a 3 (Matt).
- **⚠ THE FLAT-VS-RANKED COST GAP IS REAL, LOCALIZED, AND TWO DIFFERENT NUMBERS** — quote which statistic, or the figure means nothing.
