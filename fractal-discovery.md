## Laws
- **⚠ HEAD FLIP (ckpt 59):** every pre-ckpt-59 location-head verdict is a PRIOR. The head's TOP TIER is partition-local; its calibrated LEVEL is untrusted, its RANK within an offer is real — serve offers top-down; floors still act; an untouched place prices at the BOTTOM band. Both harvest floors cut on `P(≥3)`; the head's `P(≥4)` gates NOTHING in the walk.
- **★ THE MINABLE POPULATION IS THE LEDGER'S RATINGS (Matt, ckpt 97).** Framing is an ATTRIBUTE, never an admission ticket. **No scan, index or derived artifact may ever bound a population again** — an empty partition must appear as a zero.
- **★ MINIBROTS ARE A SOURCE — the reframe channel** (`discovery/reframing.py`). Nucleus-bound rather than seed-bound: one nucleus is one `centered` location whose rungs are its framings, the head picks the rung, the centre never moves. **`discovery/README.md §The reframing channel` owns the mechanism** — the three pin sites, the fire outcomes and which of them consume, the unresolved-root accounting, `covers`' two questions, the rung re-open rule, and how a leg decides its own priors and re-probe. Cite it; never restate it here. What belongs here instead: **nine legs exist and there is no `g3`**; `g9` and `g10` are the only ones carrying fire rows, at 1,078 barren / 374 unresolved / 36 productive and **6 / 376 / 2**; and the non-trivial `covers` branch cannot be exercised on real records because both ran the IDENTICAL ladder, so every comparison the store can make compares a ladder with itself. ⚠ **The channel is running dry** — `g10`'s split is the reading to weigh before spending a night on it. ⚠ Rung frames overwrite each other in `frames_dir` (contained; parked).
- **★ `centered` = what the parameter-plane operators find.** Every `centered` location is in a parameter plane (ZERO in julia:* and phoenix) — a partition floor can never be served by a centered arm. Centered share of a solve is MINING-INVARIANT and the one axis the solve amplifies unasked (→ MEASUREMENTS.md §sourcing channels).
- **★ SMOOTH SUPPLY ON THE PARAMETER PLANES IS SEEDED, NOT DISCOVERED**: the `proven` channel (location store AND both finished stores since ckpt 103; OFF by default, a run states it; `provenance.channel`) grows only as the stores grow. **The ckpt-103 widening was the whole of `g7`'s yield.**
- **★ A PINNED DYNAMICAL PLANE HAS NO VIRGIN-ROOT PATH** (phoenix:classic): supply = proven roots + the **viewport sampler** (`partitions.PINNED_PLANES` only). The plane OPENS but does not get BETTER (head `P(≥4)` ~0); a one-partition leg wants `--low-water 16 --cooldown 1`. **★ THE PHOENIX PLANES ARE SECONDS SHARES, NOT TURN WEIGHTS** (`curation/draw_weights.py SECONDS_SHARE`, per-(partition, BAND) price, acting since ckpt 105 — every leg before it drew both planes at 0.25 turns, at which mix the pinned plane's rows were worth negative seats at n=1000).
- Channel-specific pool laws → `preserve\sourcing_channel_laws.md`.
- **FRAMING:** 1× is a black tile for a true nucleus; 4× triage; 16× labelable; nuclei want 32× and beyond. DEGREE is never measured free of period/composition.
- **★ Record-and-rank, never gate-and-forget. Never demote a mode or channel on yield alone** — a demotion that stops a mode being mined is SELF-CONFIRMING. A pilot prices an arm's POOLED rate, never its modes; pilot the POPULATION, not the roster.
- **★ THE 2D DROP-VS-RECOLOR RULE (Matt):** hi-morph+hi-colored = drop · lo-morph+hi-colored = RECOLOR · hi-morph+lo-colored = keep · lo+lo = diverse. **Geometry is precious; palette is reassignable.**
- Allocation semantics are test-enforced (`supply/`); **floor-never-needed ≠ STARVED**; **the standing deficit is a LAUNCH SNAPSHOT**; **exploration is EVEN-CARRY over drawable partitions** (`share.weight_source = even_carry`).
- Depth is a VARIETY axis on parameter planes, a REDUNDANCY axis on dynamical ones. **Budget, not judgement, bounds exploration.** Cross-run memory = the ledgers; retention drops rows, so a dropped recipe can re-render — measured at every merge by `retention.repeat_draws`, REPORTED not prevented (ruled acceptable ckpt 108; the recipe key + `upsert` cannot double a row). A run's budget is a TARGET, not a cap.
- Framing refinement (`curation/framing.py`, ON by default) is CLOSED for centered locations. **ONE HOME: HARVEST — locations are born framed.**

## What binds a gallery
- **★ THE POOL HOLDS n.** At n=1000 and n=2000 an integer program over the view fills the gallery with every linear rule; one-per-location, group cap and family allowance refuse nothing — **but spiral DOES refuse and binds exactly**, 10.1% of seats in every run since `DEFAULT_SPIRAL_CAP` shipped 2026-09-04 (measured ckpt 114 across four seatings; → `curation/MEASUREMENTS.md`). **The greedy seed's shortfall was ORDERING** — it overspends colour and 1-swap conserves seat count, so tier 1 was frozen at the seed; "the allowance binds at every n" was a fact about the greedy's path, not the pool. The augmenting chain (ckpt 106) fills n≤1000 exhaustively; n=2000 is a BUDGET question with the chain search dominant (→ `GALLERY.md §The augmenting chain`; parked).
- **★ THE TWIN RULE: τ at 0.90× of the refit since ckpt 107 (Matt's eye on a boundary sheet), GLOBAL, one number.** The colour-AND-geometry gate is DEAD; two near-white pictures with different geometry collapse in a colour signature. **At n=1000 τ moves NO seat count** (every candidate fills), only worst/sum and which thousand. ⚠ **The twin rule holds back about a third of the view** — the rejection ledger's twin count undercounts by rule order (twin runs last); read the crossing sweep, never the ledger. **A themed pass swaps the twin test for the geometry-only `Places` rule — τ is not in the themed path.** Evidence → `GALLERY.md §The twin metric`.
- **★ AT n=2000 THE CONSTRAINTS CHOOSE THE GALLERY, NOT THE JUDGE.** The judge has no order inside its top (→ fractal-corpus); a judge flip turned over three quarters of the seats and Matt read both as fine. **Retrains buy FLOOR; the top end is Matt's eye on the final seating.**
- **★ A NEW MAP'S SEAT USUALLY TAKES A PLACE THE GALLERY WAS NOT USING, NOT ANOTHER MAP'S SEAT (ckpt 109).** Of 54 new-map seats in a fresh n=1000, **33 sat at places the baseline gallery did not hold, 17 were already seats, and 4 took the place off another row**; across seven records there were five same-place swaps in total, and distinct maps seated ROSE 528 → 542. An enlarged pool RE-SEATS rather than substitutes. **Never model new supply's effect as displacement.**
- **★ SET CONSTRAINTS COUNTED AGAINST SEATS FILLED BIND DURING THE WALK** (the spiral cap since ckpt 106; the `threads` ceiling since ckpt 108 (value → fractal-tutorial §Selection) — refusals early in the walk and a better sum at a final count under the cap). Spirals are a LOCATION property; no walk downweight (Matt). **A ceiling is Matt's instrument for any level that runs away, decided from the balance census by eye, never before.** ⚠ **Never read pool share as a seat shortage** — the angle modes are the counter-example, thin in the pool and over-seated six times a pool-mirror. A short mode is identified by ABSOLUTE rows at the bar. The full balance census, and which axes are held up by a rule rather than emergent, → `curation/GALLERY.md §Which axes are held up by a rule, and which are emergent`.
- ⚠ **A MODE'S CLEAR RATE IS BAR-DEPENDENT AND THE BAR MOVES SILENTLY** (`FALLBACK_LOCATIONS`) — fresh census before any comparison.
- **★ THE COLOUR CEILING WAS RE-PRICED AND MOVED (Matt, 2026-09-09): `ceiling.K = 3` with a new soft floor at `KF = 1`.** ⚠ Not the ONLY binding constraint, and no longer the first — the pass turns colour-bound → seat-bound between K=2.5 and 3.0, and the spiral cap now refuses where the allowance used to. **Both sweeps, their tables and every rung live in `curation/MEASUREMENTS.md`** — the 2026-09-06 section for K = 2.0–2.5 and the 2026-09-09 section for K out to 3.75. Cite them; do not restate a rung here. Two readings are worth carrying because quoting them unbounded is the exact error the measurements section exists to stop: **the "loosening buys the short cells nothing" half SURVIVES to 3.75** (14 of 16 short cells end shorter in absolute seats, four to six times further out than the first sweep), while **the TONE half REVERSES above K = 2.5** — growth from 2.5 to 3.75 is entirely vivid, +142 against −90 muted, leading split 483/516 → 559/441. **Re-sweep under the changed pool rather than re-reading a table** (`curate solve k-sweep --control`).

## Growth (`curate growth`, visit-subsampled)
**★ SEATS SATURATE; QUALITY DOES NOT.** Seats per doubling of mining decay while the seated median climbs without decay; twin collapse FALLS with pool size. "Can we seat n" (now: yes, the solve fills) and "is the gallery good" are different questions; the simple loop's "mine longer" buys the second. ## Mining economics — laws only (every rate → `curation/MEASUREMENTS.md`)
- **★ EVERY "PRIMED AT k" COUNT IS A MAXIMUM OVER k NOISY SCORES.** Quote RAW or CALIBRATED, never mixed. **★ PALETTES ARE EXCHANGEABLE WITHIN THE UNRESTRICTED POOL** to at least k=40; a carrier-restricted subset is NOT.
- **★ RARE-CELL AIMING (`--draw-cells`) IS AN n≤1000 COMPOSITION INSTRUMENT — RULED ckpt 106, not a mining policy.** ⚠ **The old "TWO scarcities, carrier-map-bound and allowance-bound" reading is RETIRED** — measured 2026-09-09, 18 of 23 falling cells are NEITHER: they finish strictly below their own allowance with more unseated clearing places than their whole deficit, and what binds is one-seat-per-cluster plus the spiral cap (→ `GALLERY.md §What actually holds a thin colour down, and it is NOT the ceiling`). Five others fall on a CO-DOMINANT cell's allowance rather than their own. **The COMPANION-ALLOWANCE effect binds untargeted cells**: a picture charges every cell it is dominant in. **Fresh places clear at ~¼ the pool's rate.** ⚠ **`color_mass` HAS EXACTLY ONE CALLER, `depth --draw-cells` (ckpt 109)** — the solve's colour machinery reads `palettes.carriers`, keyed on the MAP, so a map with no `color_mass` row is invisible to CELL AIMING and not to seating. Only **20 of the drop's 120** survive a four-thin-cell cut through the carrier prior; closing that is state's `color_mass` TODO, and it is what the `groups.jsonl` re-cut is blocked behind (cost and keying → `palettes/README.md`).
- **★ THE NEAR BAND IS THE SEAT-BUYER** — a FIRST pass over freshly opened places, CONSUMABLE, cannot be aimed at a mode; a second pass competes against ranked incumbents. **A mode's clear rate is TWO numbers, OPENER and DEEPENER.** Sized with room under `RETAIN_PER_PAIR` it nets exactly one row per free slot and the rest of its value is quality (`LEGS.md §--near-places`, measured). **★ THE BAND IS BOUNDED BY THE INCUMBENT PAIR'S ROOM, NEVER BY THE PLACE LIST (ckpt 113)** — `near_places` picks places by score and `plan_held_mode` then renders the incumbent's OWN mode, one pair per place, so the arm's channel shuts when those pairs fill even while the same places still hold slots in other modes. It is also PRICED by the incumbent's mode, so a wide roster admits composite incumbents and the band gets dearer; either bound can bind first. ⚠ **AND NEITHER IS WHAT ACTUALLY STOPPED IT (ckpt 118)** — `band.py` cuts its manifest over twelve modes while `plan_held_mode` admits only `depth.field_modes()`, three since `curvature` left the mines. Band 2 of the 2026-09-09 leg: 284 places with room, **219 with a field-mode incumbent, 87 planned**; all three band arms stopped on an EMPTY PLAN, never on the clock. One flag fixes it, `--modes smooth stripe tia` on the manifest. Breadth refills the band at **19–21%** of the places it opens, a fifth reading. ⚠ **A KEEP INCREASE CREATES A ONE-TIME STOCK OF FREE SLOTS THAT ONE LEG CAN CONSUME IN HOURS** — that is not a refill rate, and the ckpt-112 reading that priced this arm was exactly such a stock.
- **★ RECOLOURING PROVEN PLACES IS THE CHEAPEST q4 (ckpt 107–108)** — the recolour arm and the near band are the same buy and 5–25× every other arm per leg-minute; the recolour arm bought 162 of 1,000 seats in one night. One-per-location means it buys quality INSIDE a theme, never a seat in the general gallery — places still come from breadth. **★ CONDITIONING MOVES THE COLOUR COLUMN AND COSTS THE QUALITY ONE** — "CLEARED" is the wrong readout for a themed leg; read at `P(≥3) ≥ 0.50`; it is the MAP SET, not the colour; keep drawing the unconditioned control. **The aim is a ~1.8× rate cut over the unaimed control** (`LEGS.md §--draw-cells`), and its wall is CONVERSION in the judge-disliked hues, not supply or maps. **A one-hour read cannot resolve the aim in seats; an eight-hour one can** (MEASUREMENTS.md) — and eight hours buys the MIDDLE (median, seats over the bar), NOT the tail: every themed n=200 still ends in near-zero pictures. **★ SUPERSEDED AT CKPT 111: THE THIN-THEME GOAL IS A THEMED-PASS GOAL, NEVER A GENERAL-GALLERY ONE (Matt).** In the main gallery a thin colour competes on quality like any other; the aim remains the lever for a themed pass, where that cell is the whole population. ⚠ **The "no cell is owed seats" half is REVERSED as of 2026-09-09** — the colour floor guarantees every cell one fair share in the general gallery, though it stays OFF on the themed path. **The colour retention key is CLOSED, decided against** on both a seat test and a supply test → `preserve\retention_design.md`. Pruning's colour bias is real and ONE CELL WIDE — `light_vivid_lime` at 27.1% against the pool's 38.70%, while `dark_vivid_yellow`, `dark_vivid_teal` and `dark_vivid_lime` all retain ABOVE it. ⚠ Direct traps cannot be aimed by palette (`NOISY_MODES`) — give them unaimed time. ⚠ **`curvature` is UNMINED since ckpt 109** — still seated, still remodable, drawn by no leg (standing → fractal-tutorial §Mode policy). **`--draw-maps` aiming never consults the palette head**, so aiming a leg at named maps needs no model work. `direct_trap_screen` is the cheapest q4 mode on the roster and `direct_trap_lines` among the dearest — the direct traps are not one thing. A cell exclusion narrows the OFFER, not the outcome.
- **★ THE COLORING KIND IS A PRICE, NOT A ROSTER** — a field mode dumps once and recolours; composites, direct traps AND the angle modes render in full; **read `colorize.shareable`, never memory.** Retention discard is arithmetic, `(per_pair − K)/per_pair` at the shipped keep K — never a hardcoded 3.
- **★ A LEG'S WIDTH IS SIZED TO FREE SLOTS, NOT TO PLACES (ckpt 109).** A leg taking free slots runs at **width 1 and keeps every row**, where width over already-full pairs merges most of what it renders. Every count in this reading is keep-dependent and retires with K → `preserve\retention_design.md`, `curation/MEASUREMENTS.md`. **A row at a PROVEN place is worth roughly three of a row at a free slot: free slots buy PRESENCE, proven places buy SEATS.**
- **★ MINING ONE SHORT MODE WHERE MANY ARE SHORT MOVES SEATS BETWEEN THEM** — `one_per_location`; **THE THING WORTH BUYING IS PLACES.** A mine also buys seats it did not render — the enlarged pool lets the solver re-seat older rows (a third of one night's turnover). Centered places clear more often at a higher price — an aimed arm buys mode-floor seats, a centered arm buys places. ⚠ Two modes a census prices the same can be 1.9× apart measured — allocate per mode. **★ "CLEARED" SAYS NOTHING ABOUT HUMAN q3s**; a mode is never called a failure on a judge verdict alone. Novelty is worth a 3 (Matt).
- ⚠ **NOTHING SURVIVES THE MERGE THAT SAYS WHICH ARM BOUGHT A ROW** — merge between arms, sheets before merge; the pool-view door (GALLERY.md) reads a leg's seat yield after the fact by merge stamp. `PLAN_HEADROOM` means a production leg ALWAYS truncates. A scratch driver calling `depth.run` needs a `__main__` guard on Windows.
