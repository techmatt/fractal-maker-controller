## Laws
- **⚠ HEAD FLIP (ckpt 59):** every pre-ckpt-59 location-head verdict is a PRIOR. The head's TOP TIER is partition-local; its calibrated LEVEL is untrusted, its RANK within an offer is real — serve offers top-down; floors still act; an untouched place prices at the BOTTOM band. Both harvest floors cut on `P(≥3)`; the head's `P(≥4)` gates NOTHING in the walk.
- **★ THE MINABLE POPULATION IS THE LEDGER'S RATINGS (Matt, ckpt 97).** Framing is an ATTRIBUTE, never an admission ticket. **No scan, index or derived artifact may ever bound a population again** — an empty partition must appear as a zero.
- **★ MINIBROTS ARE A SOURCE — the reframe channel** (`discovery/reframing.py`). Operators fire at `proven` roots (q3+, parameter planes, eval-pinned places excluded [`test_reframing_channel.py`]); **one nucleus = ONE `centered` location whose rungs are its FRAMINGS** — the head picks the rung, the center never moves. **NEWTON-BOUND, not seed-bound** (~2 h to converge); `--reprobe` is a second sample of a spent neighbourhood; `--no-reprobe` is the plain continuation over never-fired roots. Promotions `head_q4` seeds yield q4 at ~42%, `head_keeper` ~17%. **Ladder (ckpt 107): NINE rungs 16×–256×, 192× the head's PEAK and 256× the first TURNOVER on record** (`g8`); the `width_over_root_scale` guard sits at 352.8× with 1.38× headroom — the ladder cannot double again. Every new-end head-q4 pick had a real minibrot at centre (bodies 8–16 px at 192×, 6–11 px at 256×, under the 50–100 px band); Matt ruled both rungs STAY. ⚠ **`fired` never records a barren root, and it costs**: 46% of `g8`'s seeds went to roots `g7` had fired barren, at 0.023 vs 0.301 locations a root — a continuation must record the roots it CONSUMED (owed → state OPEN 5). The channel is OPERATOR-BOUND; its wallpaper draw is the best on record (15.6% clearing at q4 vs a breadth draw's 1.7%). ⚠ A reframing leg's rung frames overwrite each other in `frames_dir` (contained: intake never resolves the name; fixing it costs ~1.4 GB of hot frames — parked).
- **★ `centered` = what the parameter-plane operators find.** Every `centered` location is in a parameter plane (ZERO in julia:* and phoenix) — a partition floor can never be served by a centered arm. Centered share of a solve is MINING-INVARIANT (0.16–0.23) and is the one axis the solve amplifies unasked.
- **★ SMOOTH SUPPLY ON THE PARAMETER PLANES IS SEEDED, NOT DISCOVERED**: the `proven` channel (location store AND both finished stores since ckpt 103; OFF by default, a run states it; `provenance.channel`) grows only as the stores grow. **The ckpt-103 widening was the whole of `g7`'s yield** — 977 of 1,518 unfired roots were finished-store roots; 0.77 locations per root, the same channel on fresh ground; 9.4 s per head-q4, the cheapest ever. `g8` is the chain's latest leg (re-found 57.5%, below the reprobe trigger); 3,616 promotions remain unfired; the location store's 541 roots are all barren since g1–g5 (`fired` cannot say so).
- **★ A PINNED DYNAMICAL PLANE HAS NO VIRGIN-ROOT PATH** (phoenix:classic): supply = proven roots + the **viewport sampler** (`partitions.PINNED_PLANES` only; ~10% survive). The plane OPENS but does not get BETTER (head `P(≥4)` ~0); a one-partition leg wants `--low-water 16 --cooldown 1`; pinned planes get 36 expansions. ~10–17× a parameter-plane candidate; price stable WITHIN A SESSION only. **★ THE PHOENIX PLANES ARE SECONDS SHARES, NOT TURN WEIGHTS** (`phoenix:classic` 3%, `phoenix` 15%; `curation/draw_weights.py SECONDS_SHARE`, per-(partition, BAND) price, EMA within the leg, unpriced band falls back to turn weights) — **acting since ckpt 105; every leg before it drew both planes at 0.25 turns**, and at that mix the pinned plane's rows are worth −5 seats at n=1000 (they clear well but land only in cells already at allowance).

- **FRAMING:** 1× is a black tile for a true nucleus; 4× triage; 16× labelable; nuclei want 32× and beyond. DEGREE is never measured free of period/composition.
- **★ Record-and-rank, never gate-and-forget. Never demote a mode or channel on yield alone** — a demotion that stops a mode being mined is SELF-CONFIRMING. A pilot prices an arm's POOLED rate, never its modes; pilot the POPULATION, not the roster.
- **★ THE 2D DROP-VS-RECOLOR RULE (Matt):** hi-morph+hi-colored = drop · lo-morph+hi-colored = RECOLOR · hi-morph+lo-colored = keep · lo+lo = diverse. **Geometry is precious; palette is reassignable.**

## What binds a gallery (rewritten ckpt 106)
- **★ THE POOL HOLDS n.** At n=1000 and n=2000 an integer program over the view fills the gallery with every linear rule in (allowance, family, group cap, floors, spiral cap); one-per-location never binds (7,702 places for ~2,000 seats); group cap, family allowance and spiral refuse nothing. **The greedy seed's shortfall was ORDERING** — it overspends colour (1.96 cells a seat against 1.80) and the 1-swap loop conserves seat count, so tier 1 was frozen at the seed. The "allowance binds at every n" reading was a fact about the greedy's path, not the pool. **The augmenting chain (shipped ckpt 106) fills n≤1000 exhaustively (117 s)**; at n=2000 the shipped stage seats 1,795 at 300 s and 1,984 at 1,800 s, neither exhausted, no mode short — a BUDGET question with the exhaustive cost unknown (>30 min); the chain search, not the diversity rule, dominates there (40% of augment time vs 91% at n=1000), so the next speedup is in the search. The old no-twin greedy was never a bound — a worse heuristic.
- **★ THE TWIN RULE: τ = 0.034281 since ckpt 107 (0.90× of the 0.03809 refit, Matt's eye on a 200-pair boundary sheet — the 88 ruled pairs were all admitted-side, 9% too close; the fit never crossed 0.5; the objective is flat below 0.85×), GLOBAL, one number.** 0.0586 refused different pictures; the colour-AND-geometry gate is DEAD (AUC 0.448); two near-white pictures with different geometry collapse in a colour signature. **At n=1000 τ moves NO seat count** (every candidate fills), only worst/sum and which thousand (158 seats turned over at 0.90×). ⚠ **The twin rule holds back ~34% of the view at τ** — the rejection ledger's twin count (41) undercounts by rule order (twin runs last); read the crossing sweep, never the ledger. **A themed pass swaps the twin test for the geometry-only `Places` rule (radius 0.07) — τ is not in the themed path.**
- **★ AT n=2000 THE CONSTRAINTS CHOOSE THE GALLERY, NOT THE JUDGE.** ~20k rows clear the bar, the judge has no order inside its top (→ fractal-corpus); a judge flip turned over three quarters of the seats for +57 net and Matt read both as fine. **Retrains buy FLOOR; the top end is Matt's eye on the final seating.**
- **★ THE SPIRAL CAP** (`ceil(0.10 × seats filled)`, counted LAST; default since ckpt 106): spirals are a LOCATION property (pool ~28%, seating 20.8% before any cap); at n=1000 the cap cost −27 net for 24% turnover; it binds to the LAST seat at every n (101/101, 180/180, 199/199); no walk downweight (Matt). **A ceiling is Matt's instrument for any level that runs away** — the same shape per mode (`threads` at 20% of seats from 3% of the pool is the first candidate), decided from the balance census by eye, never before.
- ⚠ **A MODE'S CLEAR RATE IS BAR-DEPENDENT AND THE BAR MOVES SILENTLY** (`FALLBACK_LOCATIONS` = 25) — fresh census before any comparison.

## Growth (`curate growth`, visit-subsampled)
**★ SEATS SATURATE; QUALITY DOES NOT.** Seats per doubling of mining decay while the seated median climbs without decay; a sixteenth of the mining meets every floor at n≤400; twin collapse FALLS with pool size. "Can we seat n" (now: yes, the solve fills) and "is the gallery good" are different questions; the simple loop's "mine longer" buys the second. Growth rows carry `spiral_cap` since ckpt 106 — a series silently changed meaning at that date.

## Mining economics — laws only (every rate → `curation/MEASUREMENTS.md`)
- **★ EVERY "PRIMED AT k" COUNT IS A MAXIMUM OVER k NOISY SCORES.** Quote RAW or CALIBRATED, never mixed. **★ PALETTES ARE EXCHANGEABLE WITHIN THE UNRESTRICTED POOL** to at least k=40; a carrier-restricted subset is NOT.
- **★ RARE-CELL AIMING (`--draw-cells`) IS AN n≤1000 COMPOSITION INSTRUMENT — RULED ckpt 106, not a mining policy.** `MINE_rare_cells_0904` read by counterfactual solve: at n=1000 (35 of 48 cells capped) the aimed arm bought 2× the seats and 4× the listed-cell seats of the control on matched clocks; at n=2000 (26 capped) aimed ≈ control within 12% and the listed cells went backwards. The thin cells are TWO scarcities: limes and `dark_vivid_yellow` are carrier-map-bound (forty maps clearing ~3%); magenta/rose/`dark_muted_yellow` are allowance-bound (aiming there is pointless by construction). 157 of 228 clearing places the night opened were refused by `cell_allowance` — the wall was K at that rung, and the augment stage is what moved it. **Fresh places clear at ~¼ the pool's rate.** **The COMPANION-ALLOWANCE effect binds untargeted cells**: a picture charges every cell it is dominant in (1.80 a seat), so a cell under allowance with the pool holding it (`light_vivid_purple` 28 of 42) is refused by a FULL companion cell of the same carriers, not by its own rule.
- **★ THE NEAR BAND IS THE SEAT-BUYER** — 4× the aimed arm per engine second, an order of magnitude cheaper per clear than breadth, a FIRST pass over freshly opened places; it exhausted its 88 places in 446 s of a 2,700 s cap (starved of places, which is the standing direction confirmed). It cannot be aimed at a mode and is CONSUMABLE; a second pass competes against ranked incumbents (90% pruned). **A mode's clear rate is TWO numbers, OPENER and DEEPENER.**
- **★ CONDITIONING MOVES THE COLOUR COLUMN AND COSTS THE QUALITY ONE.** "CLEARED" is the wrong readout for a themed leg — read at `P(≥3) ≥ 0.50`. It is the MAP SET, not the colour. **Keep drawing the unconditioned control.**
- **★ DEPTH SUBSTITUTES FOR RANK BUT DOES NOT REPLACE IT. CHOOSING THE MODE ROSTER IS WORTH MORE THAN RANK. MINING BUYS SEATS, NOT CEILINGS.** A judge screen on a mode the judge mis-orders selects the improvement back out (`direct_trap_multiply` variants).
- **★ THE COLORING KIND IS A PRICE, NOT A ROSTER** — a field mode dumps once and recolours; composites and direct traps render in full (~13× the seatable pictures an hour); **read `colorize.shareable`, never memory.** Retention discard is arithmetic (`(per_pair − 3)/per_pair`).
- **★ MINING ONE SHORT MODE WHERE MANY ARE SHORT MOVES SEATS BETWEEN THEM** — `one_per_location`; **THE THING WORTH BUYING IS PLACES.** Hence the standing direction (→ fractal-state). Centered places clear more often (38% vs 30%) at 2.6× the cost — an aimed arm buys mode-floor seats, a centered arm buys places. ⚠ Two modes a census prices the same can be 1.9× apart measured — allocate per mode.
- **★ "CLEARED" SAYS NOTHING ABOUT HUMAN q3s**; a mode is never called a failure on a judge verdict alone. Novelty is worth a 3 (Matt).
- **★ QUALITY, NOT COUNT, IS THE THEMED WALL (ckpt 107)**: every dark theme fills n=200; green seats 113 of 200 above the q4 bar (median 0.58), yellow 79 (0.19), the rich four 182–192. **★ RECOLOURING PROVEN PLACES IS THE CHEAPEST q4** (12.8 engine-s a kept clear vs 67 opening a fresh place vs 165 a full-render mode) and it AIMS (a 20-min arm moved green's median +0.054, controls +0.003) — but one-per-location means it buys quality INSIDE a theme, never a seat in the general gallery; places still come from breadth. `curvature` bought 5% of clears for 25% of the roster. Only `dark_vivid_yellow` (22 carriers) and dark lime (24) are CARRIER-BOUND; every other cell has the maps. **Lime/green/cyan is a judge-disliked hue** (1–5% q4 on places where rose clears 18%) — Matt: mine for the best lime WITHIN the judge, no sheet. ⚠ **A near band over a prune-free opener is EMPTY** (opener width = keep leaves nothing to deepen: kept 9.8%, exhausted in 129 s) — size the opener with room under `RETAIN_PER_PAIR`. ⚠ **Direct traps cannot be aimed by palette** (`NOISY_MODES`; 0 of 230 aimed candidates landed) — give them unaimed time. A cell exclusion narrows the OFFER, not the outcome (43% of a restricted leg's rows landed off-list). Baselines and the post-mine diff rig: `scratch/themed_baselines_0905/` (→ state).
- ⚠ **NOTHING SURVIVES THE MERGE THAT SAYS WHICH ARM BOUGHT A ROW** — merge between arms, sheets before merge; the pool-view door (GALLERY.md) reads a leg's seat yield after the fact by merge stamp. `PLAN_HEADROOM` 1.6 means a production leg ALWAYS truncates.
