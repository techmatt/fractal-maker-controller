## Laws
- **⚠ HEAD FLIP (ckpt 59):** every pre-ckpt-59 location-head verdict is a PRIOR. The head's TOP TIER is partition-local; its calibrated LEVEL is untrusted, its RANK within an offer is real — serve offers top-down; floors still act; an untouched place prices at the BOTTOM band. Both harvest floors cut on `P(≥3)`; the head's `P(≥4)` gates NOTHING in the walk.
- **★ THE MINABLE POPULATION IS THE LEDGER'S RATINGS (Matt, ckpt 97).** Framing is an ATTRIBUTE, never an admission ticket. **No scan, index or derived artifact may ever bound a population again** — an empty partition must appear as a zero.
- **★ MINIBROTS ARE A SOURCE — the reframe channel** (`discovery/reframing.py`; `discovery/README.md §The reframing channel`). Operators fire at `proven` roots (q3+, parameter planes, eval-pinned places excluded [`test_reframing_channel.py`]); **one nucleus = ONE `centered` location whose rungs are its FRAMINGS** — the head picks the rung, the center never moves. **NEWTON-BOUND, not seed-bound.** Nine rungs 16×–256× (the module docstring draws the live ladder since ckpt 108; the `width_over_root_scale` guard leaves no room to double again); Matt ruled the top rungs STAY. **★ SINCE CKPT 108 THE CHANNEL RECORDS EVERY FIRE** — `reframing_fire` rows per seed batch; outcomes `productive · barren · no_converge · not_drawn · undefined`, **only `barren` consumes** (converged, zero locations; refusal reasons that are geometry facts count as barren, `no_converge`/`orbit_escaped_immediately` do not); the ladder key is operators + rungs + `seed_max_period` read off the run header; `covers` is SPAN containment, so a rung past an end re-opens barren roots once and a rung in the middle does not; re-offered seeds sort behind every unfired seed; `--reprobe` re-fires a barren root at most once per (root, ladder) [`test_reframing_channel.py §8`]. ⚠ **NO BACKFILL IS POSSIBLE** — no earlier ledger says which seeds a leg reached before its clock, so the whole queue at ckpt 108 is `unknown` until the next leg fires. The channel's wallpaper draw is the best on record (→ MEASUREMENTS.md). ⚠ Rung frames overwrite each other in `frames_dir` (contained; parked).
- **★ `centered` = what the parameter-plane operators find.** Every `centered` location is in a parameter plane (ZERO in julia:* and phoenix) — a partition floor can never be served by a centered arm. Centered share of a solve is MINING-INVARIANT and the one axis the solve amplifies unasked (→ MEASUREMENTS.md §sourcing channels).
- **★ SMOOTH SUPPLY ON THE PARAMETER PLANES IS SEEDED, NOT DISCOVERED**: the `proven` channel (location store AND both finished stores since ckpt 103; OFF by default, a run states it; `provenance.channel`) grows only as the stores grow. **The ckpt-103 widening was the whole of `g7`'s yield.**
- **★ A PINNED DYNAMICAL PLANE HAS NO VIRGIN-ROOT PATH** (phoenix:classic): supply = proven roots + the **viewport sampler** (`partitions.PINNED_PLANES` only). The plane OPENS but does not get BETTER (head `P(≥4)` ~0); a one-partition leg wants `--low-water 16 --cooldown 1`. **★ THE PHOENIX PLANES ARE SECONDS SHARES, NOT TURN WEIGHTS** (`curation/draw_weights.py SECONDS_SHARE`, per-(partition, BAND) price, acting since ckpt 105 — every leg before it drew both planes at 0.25 turns, at which mix the pinned plane's rows were worth negative seats at n=1000).
- Channel-specific pool laws → `preserve\sourcing_channel_laws.md`.
- **FRAMING:** 1× is a black tile for a true nucleus; 4× triage; 16× labelable; nuclei want 32× and beyond. DEGREE is never measured free of period/composition.
- **★ Record-and-rank, never gate-and-forget. Never demote a mode or channel on yield alone** — a demotion that stops a mode being mined is SELF-CONFIRMING. A pilot prices an arm's POOLED rate, never its modes; pilot the POPULATION, not the roster.
- **★ THE 2D DROP-VS-RECOLOR RULE (Matt):** hi-morph+hi-colored = drop · lo-morph+hi-colored = RECOLOR · hi-morph+lo-colored = keep · lo+lo = diverse. **Geometry is precious; palette is reassignable.**
- Allocation semantics are test-enforced (`supply/`); **floor-never-needed ≠ STARVED**; **the standing deficit is a LAUNCH SNAPSHOT**; **exploration is EVEN-CARRY over drawable partitions** (`share.weight_source = even_carry`).
- Depth is a VARIETY axis on parameter planes, a REDUNDANCY axis on dynamical ones. **Budget, not judgement, bounds exploration.** Cross-run memory = the ledgers; retention drops rows, so a dropped recipe can re-render — measured at every merge by `retention.repeat_draws`, REPORTED not prevented (ruled acceptable ckpt 108; the recipe key + `upsert` cannot double a row). A run's budget is a TARGET, not a cap.
- Framing refinement (`curation/framing.py`, ON by default) is CLOSED for centered locations. **ONE HOME: HARVEST — locations are born framed.**

## What binds a gallery
- **★ THE POOL HOLDS n.** At n=1000 and n=2000 an integer program over the view fills the gallery with every linear rule; one-per-location, group cap, family allowance and spiral refuse nothing. **The greedy seed's shortfall was ORDERING** — it overspends colour and 1-swap conserves seat count, so tier 1 was frozen at the seed; "the allowance binds at every n" was a fact about the greedy's path, not the pool. The augmenting chain (ckpt 106) fills n≤1000 exhaustively; n=2000 is a BUDGET question with the chain search dominant (→ `GALLERY.md §The augmenting chain`; parked).
- **★ THE TWIN RULE: τ at 0.90× of the refit since ckpt 107 (Matt's eye on a boundary sheet), GLOBAL, one number.** The colour-AND-geometry gate is DEAD; two near-white pictures with different geometry collapse in a colour signature. **At n=1000 τ moves NO seat count** (every candidate fills), only worst/sum and which thousand. ⚠ **The twin rule holds back about a third of the view** — the rejection ledger's twin count undercounts by rule order (twin runs last); read the crossing sweep, never the ledger. **A themed pass swaps the twin test for the geometry-only `Places` rule — τ is not in the themed path.** Evidence → `GALLERY.md §The twin metric`.
- **★ AT n=2000 THE CONSTRAINTS CHOOSE THE GALLERY, NOT THE JUDGE.** The judge has no order inside its top (→ fractal-corpus); a judge flip turned over three quarters of the seats and Matt read both as fine. **Retrains buy FLOOR; the top end is Matt's eye on the final seating.**
- **★ A NEW MAP'S SEAT USUALLY TAKES A PLACE THE GALLERY WAS NOT USING, NOT ANOTHER MAP'S SEAT (ckpt 109).** Of 54 new-map seats in a fresh n=1000, **33 sat at places the baseline gallery did not hold, 17 were already seats, and 4 took the place off another row**; across seven records there were five same-place swaps in total, and distinct maps seated ROSE 528 → 542. An enlarged pool RE-SEATS rather than substitutes. **Never model new supply's effect as displacement.**
- **★ SET CONSTRAINTS COUNTED AGAINST SEATS FILLED BIND DURING THE WALK** (the spiral cap since ckpt 106; the `threads` ceiling since ckpt 108 (value → fractal-tutorial §Selection) — refusals early in the walk and a better sum at a final count under the cap). Spirals are a LOCATION property; no walk downweight (Matt). **A ceiling is Matt's instrument for any level that runs away, decided from the balance census by eye, never before.** ⚠ **The angle modes are the counter-example: thin in the pool, OVER-seated in the gallery** (six times a pool-mirror at 1% of pool) — never read pool share as a seat shortage; read the census's pool-mirror column (rig kept in `scratch/overnight_8h_0905/census/`, → state).
- ⚠ **A MODE'S CLEAR RATE IS BAR-DEPENDENT AND THE BAR MOVES SILENTLY** (`FALLBACK_LOCATIONS`) — fresh census before any comparison.

## Growth (`curate growth`, visit-subsampled)
**★ SEATS SATURATE; QUALITY DOES NOT.** Seats per doubling of mining decay while the seated median climbs without decay; twin collapse FALLS with pool size. "Can we seat n" (now: yes, the solve fills) and "is the gallery good" are different questions; the simple loop's "mine longer" buys the second. Growth rows carry `spiral_cap` since ckpt 106 and `mode_ceilings` since ckpt 108 — a series silently changed meaning at those dates.

## Mining economics — laws only (every rate → `curation/MEASUREMENTS.md`)
- **★ EVERY "PRIMED AT k" COUNT IS A MAXIMUM OVER k NOISY SCORES.** Quote RAW or CALIBRATED, never mixed. **★ PALETTES ARE EXCHANGEABLE WITHIN THE UNRESTRICTED POOL** to at least k=40; a carrier-restricted subset is NOT.
- **★ RARE-CELL AIMING (`--draw-cells`) IS AN n≤1000 COMPOSITION INSTRUMENT — RULED ckpt 106, not a mining policy.** The thin cells are TWO scarcities: carrier-map-bound (`dark_vivid_yellow`, dark lime) and allowance-bound (aiming there is pointless by construction); the augment stage is what moved the allowance wall. **The COMPANION-ALLOWANCE effect binds untargeted cells**: a picture charges every cell it is dominant in. **Fresh places clear at ~¼ the pool's rate.** ⚠ **`color_mass` HAS EXACTLY ONE CALLER, `depth --draw-cells` (ckpt 109)** — the solve's colour machinery reads `palettes.carriers`, keyed on the MAP, so a map with no `color_mass` row is invisible to CELL AIMING and not to seating. Only **20 of the drop's 120** survive a four-thin-cell cut through the carrier prior; closing that is state's `color_mass` TODO, and it is what the `groups.jsonl` re-cut is blocked behind (cost and keying → `palettes/README.md`).
- **★ THE NEAR BAND IS THE SEAT-BUYER** — a FIRST pass over freshly opened places, CONSUMABLE, cannot be aimed at a mode; a second pass competes against ranked incumbents. **A mode's clear rate is TWO numbers, OPENER and DEEPENER.** Sized with room under `RETAIN_PER_PAIR` it nets exactly one row per free slot and the rest of its value is quality (`LEGS.md §--near-places`, measured).
- **★ RECOLOURING PROVEN PLACES IS THE CHEAPEST q4 (ckpt 107–108)** — the recolour arm and the near band are the same buy and 5–25× every other arm per leg-minute; the recolour arm bought 162 of 1,000 seats in one night. One-per-location means it buys quality INSIDE a theme, never a seat in the general gallery — places still come from breadth. **★ CONDITIONING MOVES THE COLOUR COLUMN AND COSTS THE QUALITY ONE** — "CLEARED" is the wrong readout for a themed leg; read at `P(≥3) ≥ 0.50`; it is the MAP SET, not the colour; keep drawing the unconditioned control. **The aim is a ~1.8× rate cut over the unaimed control** (`LEGS.md §--draw-cells`), and its wall is CONVERSION in the judge-disliked hues, not supply or maps. **A one-hour read cannot resolve the aim in seats; an eight-hour one can** (MEASUREMENTS.md) — and eight hours buys the MIDDLE (median, seats over the bar), NOT the tail: every themed n=200 still ends in near-zero pictures. Dropping the lime/green/cyan/teal aim was the WRONG conclusion — thin-theme quality is an active goal and the aim is its lever (Matt, ckpt 108). ⚠ Direct traps cannot be aimed by palette (`NOISY_MODES`) — give them unaimed time. ⚠ **`curvature` is UNMINED since ckpt 109** — still seated, still remodable, drawn by no leg (standing → fractal-tutorial §Mode policy). **`--draw-maps` aiming never consults the palette head**, so aiming a leg at named maps needs no model work. `direct_trap_screen` is the cheapest q4 mode on the roster and `direct_trap_lines` among the dearest — the direct traps are not one thing. A cell exclusion narrows the OFFER, not the outcome.
- **★ THE COLORING KIND IS A PRICE, NOT A ROSTER** — a field mode dumps once and recolours; composites, direct traps AND the angle modes render in full; **read `colorize.shareable`, never memory.** Retention discard is arithmetic (`(per_pair − 3)/per_pair`).
- **★ `smooth`'s HIGH-BAND POPULATION IS FULL, SO A LEG'S WIDTH IS SIZED TO FREE SLOTS (ckpt 109).** Of 6,537 high-band places, **5,682 already hold the full keep of three at `smooth`** and 80 have never been tried; its whole prune-free population was 2,237 slots over 1,510 places. A leg taking those slots runs at **width 1 and keeps every row**, where width 3 over full pairs merges about a tenth. **A row at a PROVEN place is worth roughly three of a row at a free slot** — 5,091 sheet-leg rows over 600 proven places seated 74 across seven records against 10,121 mine rows seating 90. Free slots buy PRESENCE; proven places buy SEATS.
- **★ MINING ONE SHORT MODE WHERE MANY ARE SHORT MOVES SEATS BETWEEN THEM** — `one_per_location`; **THE THING WORTH BUYING IS PLACES.** A mine also buys seats it did not render — the enlarged pool lets the solver re-seat older rows (a third of one night's turnover). Centered places clear more often at a higher price — an aimed arm buys mode-floor seats, a centered arm buys places. ⚠ Two modes a census prices the same can be 1.9× apart measured — allocate per mode. **★ "CLEARED" SAYS NOTHING ABOUT HUMAN q3s**; a mode is never called a failure on a judge verdict alone. Novelty is worth a 3 (Matt).
- ⚠ **NOTHING SURVIVES THE MERGE THAT SAYS WHICH ARM BOUGHT A ROW** — merge between arms, sheets before merge; the pool-view door (GALLERY.md) reads a leg's seat yield after the fact by merge stamp. `PLAN_HEADROOM` means a production leg ALWAYS truncates. A scratch driver calling `depth.run` needs a `__main__` guard on Windows.
