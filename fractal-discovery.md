## Laws
- **⚠ HEAD FLIP (ckpt 59):** every pre-ckpt-59 location-head verdict is a PRIOR. The head's TOP TIER is partition-local (never pool or compare its 4s across partitions); its calibrated LEVEL is untrusted, its RANK within an offer is real — serve offers top-down; floors still act. Its per-rung LEVEL on one nucleus swings orders of magnitude while its PICK is stable; its rank pays WITHIN its own top tier (top band 2.3× the bottom band's seatable rate), so an untouched place prices at the BOTTOM band, never the leg's mean. Both harvest floors cut on `P(≥3)`; the head's `P(≥4)` gates NOTHING in the walk — a partition with almost no q4 LOCATION labels (phoenix:classic: 1 in 73 there; the finished stores hold 50 q4s on the plane) is admitted, drawn and ranked among its own kind regardless.
- **★ THE MINABLE POPULATION IS THE LEDGER'S RATINGS (Matt, ckpt 97).** Minable = head rating over the junk floor + embedded + no candidate row on it. **Framing is an ATTRIBUTE, never an admission ticket**; absence is not rejection. The old scan gate hid 8,015 never-opened locations, 329 of them ordinary walk locations. **No scan, index or derived artifact may ever bound a population again** — and an empty partition must appear as a zero, not as absence (the phoenix:classic exclusion was invisible for exactly that reason).
- **★ MINIBROTS ARE A SOURCE — the reframe channel** (`discovery/reframing.py`; the overturn story → settled_rulings §ckpt 101). Operators fire at `proven` roots (q3+, parameter planes, eval-pinned places excluded as seed and as frame [`test_reframing_channel.py`]); **one nucleus = ONE `centered` location whose rungs are its FRAMINGS** — the head picks the rung, the center never moves. **The channel is NEWTON-BOUND, not seed-bound**: the promotion queue converges in ~2 h; `--reprobe` is a second sample of a spent neighbourhood needing no new label; `--prior` must name EVERY earlier leg. Promotions: `head_q4` seeds yield q4 at ~42%, `head_keeper` at ~17%, and firing BOTH classes is what makes a night; every q4 in the channel is HEAD-ON-HEAD until Matt looks (labels only on divergence). **Rungs: the head-q4 rate plateaus at 32×**; the ladder is NINE rungs 8×–128×; read `old_ends.share` against 37.3% before extending; the render judge falls away by 64×. The channel is OPERATOR-BOUND (`expand_neighborhood` ≈ the whole clock), so the render pool does not apply to it. Its wallpaper draw is the best on record (15.6% clearing at q4 against a breadth draw's 1.7% — a population effect).
- **★ `centered` = what the parameter-plane operators find.** Every `centered` location is in a parameter plane (multibrot5 > 4 > 3 > mandelbrot; ZERO in julia:* and phoenix) — a partition floor can never be served by a centered arm. What shapes the gallery is K, and K stands (Matt, ruled).
- **★ THE TWIN RULE AT τ=0.0586 WAS REFUSING DIFFERENT PICTURES** (ruled 0.65× → 0.03809, GLOBAL): below 0.60 τ every refused pair reads as one wallpaper, above 0.65 plainly two; 66% of refusals sat above 0.65. n=2000 1,033 → 1,569 seats, shortfall 68 → 7, 500 old seats moved; refusals 3× fewer and flatter (heaviest seat 105 → 29). **The colour-AND-geometry gate is DEAD (measured):** the neutral-embedding distance has NO duplicate-detection power (AUC 0.448). Widening τ can never add a seat; price it by a vertex-cover bound. **Two near-white pictures with different geometry collapse in a colour signature** — the `direct_trap_multiply` whitewash (engine item, LATER) is why its 7-seat shortfall is refused by `location` and `cell_allowance` rather than supply.
- **★ DISTINCTNESS AND ONE-PER-LOCATION BIND, NOT SUPPLY.** At n=2000 under v6 (ckpt 103) NOTHING is short (`short_total` 0; five modes exactly on their floor); `cell_allowance` 9,790, one-per-location 3,213, twins 1,951 — **and the refusal class splits on MODE COST**: allowance dominates the shareable field modes (deep supply losing to the colour ceiling), one-per-location dominates all nine dear modes (clearing rows piling up at places something else holds), twins bind nothing. `--floor-seats 2000` (distinct PLACES over the bar, ledger-wide) is short in 12 of 14 modes — the dear modes have 100–230 clearing places each. Read the refusal before buying supply for it; **a refusal reading is PER MODE, not global** — never carry one mode's profile to another.
- **★ AT n=2000 THE CONSTRAINTS CHOOSE THE GALLERY, NOT THE JUDGE (ckpt 103).** ~20k rows clear the bar, ~1,650 seat, and the judge has no order inside its top (→ fractal-corpus): a judge flip turned over three quarters of the seats for +57 net (pool growth alone moves ~100) and Matt's eye read both seatings as fine. ## Mining economics — laws only (every rate → `curation/README.md`)
- **★ EVERY "PRIMED AT k" COUNT IS A MAXIMUM OVER k NOISY SCORES AND IS OPTIMISTIC.** Quote RAW or CALIBRATED, never mixed.
- **★ PALETTES ARE EXCHANGEABLE WITHIN THE UNRESTRICTED POOL** to at least k=40; a carrier-restricted subset is NOT. High k dominates for seat-hunting and is exactly wrong when a one-per-location sheet is the product.
- **★ CONDITIONING MOVES THE COLOUR COLUMN AND COSTS THE QUALITY ONE — GRADE-DEPENDENT.** "CLEARED" (`P(≥4) ≥ 0.50`) is the WRONG readout for a themed leg — read it at `P(≥3) ≥ 0.50`. **It is the MAP SET, not the colour**: carriers are worse maps; the carrier table is a prior, not a gate. **Aiming the palette buys a thin cell 19–50× cheaper than the census prices it** (the census row is the cost of WAITING for a colour) — but aimed supply moved supply, not seats (cell spread unmoved at 7.64×): the allowance, not supply, is the wall. **A leg-wide map exclusion by full-cell mass (`curate depth --draw-maps`; drop the maps whose `color_mass` leans to the cells at allowance — 406 of 822 at the 0.60 median cut) is a COMPOSITION instrument, not a throughput one** (OPEN share of clears 80% vs 50% unfiltered; OPEN clears per second unchanged), and "OPEN" spans 25 cells from two-under-allowance to empty, so a coarse cut fills the popular end. **The fine instrument is a per-draw CELL ask through the carriers** (Matt's design → fractal-state OPEN 1) — unbuilt; the near band is where a recolour is cheap.
- **⚠ THE LEDGER-WIDE CLEAR RATE IS NOT A DRAW RATE**; rates are ROSTER FIGURES; an AIMED leg's rows raise a ledger-wide rate into a CEILING. **Rank lift depends on the roster** (1.58× on a dear roster, 1.31× on a field one). **Keep drawing the unconditioned control** — it proves a ranked arm earned its keep.
- **★ DEPTH SUBSTITUTES FOR RANK BUT DOES NOT REPLACE IT.** **CHOOSING THE MODE ROSTER IS WORTH MORE THAN RANK.** Only `best_rank` separates a real move from the denominator. **★ MINING BUYS SEATS, NOT CEILINGS.** **A judge screen on a mode the judge mis-orders selects the improvement back out** — the `direct_trap_multiply` variants read flat under `P(≥3)` and 2.1× matched (→ fractal-corpus §Judge method). **★ THE NEAR BAND IS AN ORDER OF MAGNITUDE CHEAPER PER CLEAR THAN BREADTH** (ckpt 103: 0.42 vs 3.96 engine-s a candidate, 24× clears per second, 84 of a leg's 153 new seats on a tenth of its clock — one dump amortises over the set; a `--rate` carried in from breadth sizes it at a quarter of what its hour buys). It cannot be aimed at a mode [`depth.plan_held_mode`] and is CONSUMABLE. **A mode's clear rate is TWO numbers, OPENER and DEEPENER** (`curvature` 2% in breadth, 30% in the near band — a roster treating them as one throws the second away); `threads` is the best floor buy (21%, 1,568 places short). **Weighting seconds toward the modes furthest under their floor costs efficiency** — they are furthest under because they clear worst. **Centering is a loss at matched modes** (2.5% vs 5.8%).
- **★ THE COLORING KIND IS A PRICE, NOT A ROSTER.** A field mode dumps once and recolours; composites and direct traps render in full (~13× the seatable pictures an hour for field over each) — PRICE it, never hard-code which modes a leg may draw. **★ NEVER ASSERT A MODE'S KIND FROM MEMORY — read `colorize.shareable`.** A declared share IS a share of seconds when each arm is its own wall-clock leg; the counts-vs-seconds trap is `weave` inside one plan. Retention discard is arithmetic (`(per_pair − 3)/per_pair`; 3 is free; breadth over modes is prune-free).
- **★ MINING ONE SHORT MODE WHERE MANY ARE SHORT MOVES SEATS BETWEEN THEM** — `one_per_location` is the mechanism; **THE THING WORTH BUYING IS PLACES.** Hence the standing direction (Matt, ckpt 103): **general mining = breadth to OPEN places (full roster, a large cheap smooth/field share — ≥50% is fine), the near band to make them pay, the dear kinds on the floor draw** (a field-only leg is DISJOINT from every short mode; the nine-dear-mode roster closed four floors in one night, confirmed). Centered places clear more often (38% vs 30%) and cost 2.6× per clearing place on a dear roster — an aimed arm buys mode-floor seats, a centered arm buys places. ⚠ Two modes a census prices the same can be 1.9× apart measured — allocate per mode, never per pair.
- **★ "CLEARED" SAYS NOTHING ABOUT HUMAN q3s**; a mode is never called a failure on a judge verdict alone (Matt). Novelty is worth a 3 (Matt).
- ⚠ **NOTHING SURVIVES THE MERGE THAT SAYS WHICH ARM BOUGHT A ROW** — an arm's yield is unreadable after the fact; merge between arms, sheets before merge. `PLAN_HEADROOM` 1.6 means a production leg ALWAYS truncates. A scratch driver calling `depth.run` needs a `__main__` guard on Windows.
