## Mining economics — laws only (every rate → `curation/README.md`)
- **★ EVERY "PRIMED AT k" COUNT IS A MAXIMUM OVER k NOISY SCORES AND IS OPTIMISTIC.** Quote a prime count as RAW or CALIBRATED, never mixed.
- **★ PALETTES ARE EXCHANGEABLE WITHIN THE UNRESTRICTED POOL** to at least k=40 — the palette head's rank buys nothing at the boundary and k is bounded by cost alone. ⚠ **A CARRIER-RESTRICTED SUBSET IS NOT EXCHANGEABLE WITH IT.** **High k strictly dominates for seat-hunting and is exactly wrong when a one-per-location sheet is the product.**
- **★ CONDITIONING MOVES THE COLOUR COLUMN AND COSTS THE QUALITY ONE — GRADE-DEPENDENT (ckpt 96).** A conditioned lime arm hit its cell 44.8% against the control's 0.78% (57.6× lift, 22 of 24 carriers), cleared NOTHING at the q4 crossing, and was the CHEAPEST arm in the leg per q3 location at the acting bar. **"CLEARED" (a `P(≥4) ≥ 0.50` crossing) is the WRONG READOUT for a themed leg — read it at `P(≥3) ≥ 0.50`.** Conditioning is mode-dependent by design: it delivers where the COLORMAP makes the colour, not where a TRAP does.
- **★ IT IS THE MAP SET, NOT THE COLOUR.** Carriers are worse maps whatever colour they land on; a themed collection is bought by paying for a much worse score distribution and taking its thin top — draw biased, render, read the dominance the row already carries, keep what came out dominant; the carrier table is a prior, not a gate. **A conditioned mine cannot buy a themed gallery at shipping size** (ckpt 99): the wall was the group cap — now the themed cap, then the geometry radius; details ONE-HOME at fractal-tutorial §Selection. The colour-cloud twin metric on a themed pool was measuring the theme.
- **⚠ THE LEDGER-WIDE CLEAR RATE IS NOT A DRAW RATE** — a fresh breadth draw on never-opened locations is what a new arm is read against. **⚠ Rates are ROSTER FIGURES, not constants.** ⚠ An AIMED leg's rows raise a ledger-wide per-mode rate into a CEILING (ckpt 97's three cost pilots, and `mode_policy`'s own head-top table).
- **★ DEPTH SUBSTITUTES FOR RANK BUT DOES NOT REPLACE IT.** Draw the top half, not the whole range. **CHOOSING THE MODE ROSTER IS WORTH MORE THAN RANK.**
- **★ A BEST-AVAILABLE PERCENTILE IS NOT A MINING READOUT.** **Only `best_rank` separates a real move from the denominator.**
- **★ MINING BUYS SEATS, NOT CEILINGS.** At n=150 the tail is what binds, so that is the right purchase — where a tail exists to buy; where the cap binds, nothing does.
- **★ THE NEAR BAND IS ~20× BREADTH IN SEATS PER WALL HOUR** but cannot be aimed at a mode [ENFORCED: `depth.plan_held_mode`] and is CONSUMABLE. ⚠ **`--band-weights` with the lower bands at 0 is the flag that cuts the ranked draw, not `--top-bands`.**
- **★ THE COLORING KIND IS A PRICE, NOT A ROSTER (ckpt 97).** A field mode dumps once and recolours; a composite and a direct trap render in full every candidate — measured at roughly 13.9× and 12.7× the seatable pictures an hour for field over each. That difference is to be PRICED and paid where the seats are needed, never hard-coded as which modes a leg may draw. ⚠ A share is currently a share of the PLAN's count, not of its budget — a 5%-declared arm took 25% of the seconds. ⚠ **NEVER ASSERT A MODE'S KIND FROM MEMORY — read `colorize.shareable`.** `smooth_mean_angle` and `smooth_angle_min` were twice called field modes in prompts and are COMPOSITES, the dearest on record after `smooth_stripe` at ~5–8 s a candidate.
- **★ MINING ONE SHORT MODE IN A GALLERY WHERE MANY ARE SHORT MOVES SEATS BETWEEN THEM (ckpt 98).** A half-hour leg aimed at the two emptiest modes bought them +4 seats each and `direct_trap_screen` +2, while `smooth_curvature` lost 6, `smooth_stripe` 3 and `threads` 1 — **net shortfall 200 → 200.** `one_per_location` is the mechanism: a place seated in one short mode is a place not seated in another, and 16 new clearing locations added only NINE places to the view. **THE THING WORTH BUYING IS PLACES.** Hence the standing direction, SHARPENED ckpt 99 (Matt): **general mining = FULL-ROSTER legs — most seconds on the dear (non-shareable) kinds, a cheap field share retained** — a field-only `depth` leg is DISJOINT from every short mode by construction (all nine short at n=2000 are non-shareable; proven: 55 new places, +11 seats, ZERO floor movement — a new place serves a floor only when the dear mode is attempted there). The head's rank buys little on field modes (1.07× against its 1.5–2.2× on dear kinds — keep drawing the unconditioned control either way).
- ⚠ **A REFUSAL READING IS PER MODE, NOT GLOBAL.** For those two modes SUPPLY refuses — 963 of 979 candidates failed their own mode's bar and never entered the pool, and `one_per_location` refuses 2–3× what twin does. Gallery-wide the opposite holds (twin 2,785 locations against one-per-location's 663). Never carry one mode's refusal profile to another, or the gallery's to a mode.
