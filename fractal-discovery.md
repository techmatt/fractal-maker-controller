## Laws
- **⚠ HEAD FLIP (ckpt 59):** every pre-ckpt-59 location-head verdict is a PRIOR. The head's TOP TIER is partition-local; its calibrated LEVEL is untrusted, its RANK within an offer is real — serve offers top-down; floors still act; an untouched place prices at the BOTTOM band. Both harvest floors cut on `P(≥3)`; the head's `P(≥4)` gates NOTHING in the walk.
- **★ THE MINABLE POPULATION IS THE LEDGER'S RATINGS (Matt, ckpt 97).** Framing is an ATTRIBUTE, never an admission ticket. **No scan, index or derived artifact may ever bound a population again** — an empty partition must appear as a zero.
- **★ MINIBROTS ARE A SOURCE — the reframe channel** (`discovery/reframing.py`). Operators fire at `proven` roots (q3+, parameter planes, eval-pinned places excluded [`test_reframing_channel.py`]); **one nucleus = ONE `centered` location whose rungs are its FRAMINGS** — the head picks the rung, the center never moves. **NEWTON-BOUND, not seed-bound** (~2 h to converge); `--reprobe` is a second sample of a spent neighbourhood; `--no-reprobe` is the plain continuation over never-fired roots. Promotions `head_q4` seeds yield q4 at ~42%, `head_keeper` ~17%. **Ladder (ckpt 106): NINE rungs 8×–128×, and 128× IS THE WALL** — on `g7` it took the most picks (20.5%) and the most head-q4 (39%) of any rung while 8× and 12× bought nothing; the old "plateaus at 32×" was a five-rung reading and the old-ends reference (37.3%) read 21.1% on nine rungs. Matt ruled the extension (drop 8×/12×, add 192×/256× — queued `LADDER_extend_0904`); the caveat is framing: the body is 14–23 px at 128×, under Matt's 50–100 px nucleus band, so new-end picks may be filament frames — his eye decides, not the head. The channel is OPERATOR-BOUND; its wallpaper draw is the best on record (15.6% clearing at q4 vs a breadth draw's 1.7%). ⚠ **`fired` never records a barren root** — a re-offer of `g6`'s empty roots returned exactly zero on `g7`; "every proven root has fired" is never true, and a continuation over "unfired" roots must subtract the barren ones by hand. ⚠ A reframing leg's rung frames overwrite each other in `frames_dir` (contained: intake never resolves the name; fixing it costs ~1.4 GB of hot frames — parked).
- **★ `centered` = what the parameter-plane operators find.** Every `centered` location is in a parameter plane (ZERO in julia:* and phoenix) — a partition floor can never be served by a centered arm. Centered share of a solve is MINING-INVARIANT (0.16–0.23) and is the one axis the solve amplifies unasked.
- **★ SMOOTH SUPPLY ON THE PARAMETER PLANES IS SEEDED, NOT DISCOVERED**: the `proven` channel (location store AND both finished stores since ckpt 103; OFF by default, a run states it; `provenance.channel`) grows only as the stores grow. **The ckpt-103 widening was the whole of `g7`'s yield** — 977 of 1,518 unfired roots were finished-store roots; 0.77 locations per root, the same channel on fresh ground; 9.4 s per head-q4, the cheapest ever. `g7` is the chain's latest leg (re-found 23.9% — unspent); 1,263 roots and 3,616 promotions (1,052 `head_q4`) remain unfired.
- **★ A PINNED DYNAMICAL PLANE HAS NO VIRGIN-ROOT PATH** (phoenix:classic): supply = proven roots + the **viewport sampler** (`partitions.PINNED_PLANES` only; ~10% survive). The plane OPENS but does not get BETTER (head `P(≥4)` ~0); a one-partition leg wants `--low-water 16 --cooldown 1`; pinned planes get 36 expansions. ~10–17× a parameter-plane candidate; price stable WITHIN A SESSION only. **★ THE PHOENIX PLANES ARE SECONDS SHARES, NOT TURN WEIGHTS** (`phoenix:classic` 3%, `phoenix` 15%; `curation/draw_weights.py SECONDS_SHARE`, per-(partition, BAND) price, EMA within the leg, unpriced band falls back to turn weights) — **acting since ckpt 105; every leg before it drew both planes at 0.25 turns**, and at that mix the pinned plane's rows are worth −5 seats at n=1000 (they clear well but land only in cells already at allowance).
- Channel-specific pool laws → `preserve\sourcing_channel_laws.md`.
- **FRAMING:** 1× is a black tile for a true nucleus; 4× triage; 16× labelable; nuclei want 32× and beyond. DEGREE is never measured free of period/composition.
- **★ Record-and-rank, never gate-and-forget. Never demote a mode or channel on yield alone** — a demotion that stops a mode being mined is SELF-CONFIRMING. A pilot prices an arm's POOLED rate, never its modes; pilot the POPULATION, not the roster.
- **★ THE 2D DROP-VS-RECOLOR RULE (Matt):** hi-morph+hi-colored = drop · lo-morph+hi-colored = RECOLOR · hi-morph+lo-colored = keep · lo+lo = diverse. **Geometry is precious; palette is reassignable.**
- Allocation semantics are test-enforced (`supply/`); **floor-never-needed ≠ STARVED**; **the standing deficit is a LAUNCH SNAPSHOT**; **exploration is EVEN-CARRY over drawable partitions** (`share.weight_source = even_carry`).
- Depth is a VARIETY axis on parameter planes, a REDUNDANCY axis on dynamical ones. **Budget, not judgement, bounds exploration.** Cross-run memory = the ledgers (retention drops rows, so a dropped recipe re-renders). A run's budget is a TARGET, not a cap.
- Framing refinement (`curation/framing.py`, ON by default) is CLOSED for centered locations. **ONE HOME: HARVEST — locations are born framed.**

## What binds a gallery (rewritten ckpt 106)
- **★ THE POOL HOLDS n.** At n=1000 and n=2000 an integer program over the view fills the gallery with every linear rule in (allowance, family, group cap, floors, spiral cap); one-per-location never binds (7,702 places for ~2,000 seats); group cap, family allowance and spiral refuse nothing. **The greedy seed's shortfall was ORDERING** — it overspends colour (1.96 cells a seat against 1.80) and the 1-swap loop conserves seat count, so tier 1 was frozen at the seed. The "allowance binds at every n" reading was a fact about the greedy's path, not the pool. **The augmenting chain (shipped ckpt 106) fills n≤1000 with the twin test on**; n=2000 measured +309 of 379 on the prototype with 70 unknown (search not exhausted). The old no-twin greedy was never a bound — a worse heuristic.
- **★ THE TWIN RULE AT τ=0.0586 WAS REFUSING DIFFERENT PICTURES** (ruled 0.03809, GLOBAL); the colour-AND-geometry gate is DEAD (neutral-embedding distance has no duplicate-detection power, AUC 0.448); two near-white pictures with different geometry collapse in a colour signature.
- **★ AT n=2000 THE CONSTRAINTS CHOOSE THE GALLERY, NOT THE JUDGE.** ~20k rows clear the bar, the judge has no order inside its top (→ fractal-corpus); a judge flip turned over three quarters of the seats for +57 net and Matt read both as fine. **Retrains buy FLOOR; the top end is Matt's eye on the final seating.**
- **★ THE SPIRAL CAP** (`ceil(0.10 × seats filled)`, counted LAST; default since ckpt 106): spirals are a LOCATION property (pool ~28%, seating 20.8% before any cap); at n=1000 the cap cost −27 net for 24% turnover; no walk downweight (Matt).
- ⚠ **A MODE'S CLEAR RATE IS BAR-DEPENDENT AND THE BAR MOVES SILENTLY** (`FALLBACK_LOCATIONS` = 25) — fresh census before any comparison.

## Growth (`curate growth`, visit-subsampled)
**★ SEATS SATURATE; QUALITY DOES NOT.** Seats per doubling of mining decay while the seated median climbs without decay; a sixteenth of the mining meets every floor at n≤400; twin collapse FALLS with pool size. "Can we seat n" (now: yes, the solve fills) and "is the gallery good" are different questions; the simple loop's "mine longer" buys the second. Growth rows carry `spiral_cap` since ckpt 106 — a series silently changed meaning at that date.

## Mining economics — laws only (every rate → `curation/MEASUREMENTS.md`)
- **★ EVERY "PRIMED AT k" COUNT IS A MAXIMUM OVER k NOISY SCORES.** Quote RAW or CALIBRATED, never mixed. **★ PALETTES ARE EXCHANGEABLE WITHIN THE UNRESTRICTED POOL** to at least k=40; a carrier-restricted subset is NOT.
- **★ RARE-CELL AIMING (`--draw-cells`) IS AN n≤1000 COMPOSITION INSTRUMENT — RULED ckpt 106, not a mining policy.** `MINE_rare_cells_0904` read by counterfactual solve: at n=1000 (35 of 48 cells capped) the aimed arm bought 2× the seats and 4× the listed-cell seats of the control on matched clocks; at n=2000 (26 capped) aimed ≈ control within 12% and the listed cells went backwards. The thin cells are TWO scarcities: limes and `dark_vivid_yellow` are carrier-map-bound (forty maps clearing ~3%); magenta/rose/`dark_muted_yellow` are allowance-bound (aiming there is pointless by construction). 157 of 228 clearing places the night opened were refused by `cell_allowance` — the wall was K at that rung, and the augment stage is what moved it. **Fresh places clear at ~¼ the pool's rate.**
- **★ THE NEAR BAND IS THE SEAT-BUYER** — 4× the aimed arm per engine second, an order of magnitude cheaper per clear than breadth, a FIRST pass over freshly opened places; it exhausted its 88 places in 446 s of a 2,700 s cap (starved of places, which is the standing direction confirmed). It cannot be aimed at a mode and is CONSUMABLE; a second pass competes against ranked incumbents (90% pruned). **A mode's clear rate is TWO numbers, OPENER and DEEPENER.**
- **★ CONDITIONING MOVES THE COLOUR COLUMN AND COSTS THE QUALITY ONE.** "CLEARED" is the wrong readout for a themed leg — read at `P(≥3) ≥ 0.50`. It is the MAP SET, not the colour. **Keep drawing the unconditioned control.**
- **★ DEPTH SUBSTITUTES FOR RANK BUT DOES NOT REPLACE IT. CHOOSING THE MODE ROSTER IS WORTH MORE THAN RANK. MINING BUYS SEATS, NOT CEILINGS.** A judge screen on a mode the judge mis-orders selects the improvement back out (`direct_trap_multiply` variants).
- **★ THE COLORING KIND IS A PRICE, NOT A ROSTER** — a field mode dumps once and recolours; composites and direct traps render in full (~13× the seatable pictures an hour); **read `colorize.shareable`, never memory.** Retention discard is arithmetic (`(per_pair − 3)/per_pair`).
- **★ MINING ONE SHORT MODE WHERE MANY ARE SHORT MOVES SEATS BETWEEN THEM** — `one_per_location`; **THE THING WORTH BUYING IS PLACES.** Hence the standing direction (→ fractal-state). Centered places clear more often (38% vs 30%) at 2.6× the cost — an aimed arm buys mode-floor seats, a centered arm buys places. ⚠ Two modes a census prices the same can be 1.9× apart measured — allocate per mode.
- **★ "CLEARED" SAYS NOTHING ABOUT HUMAN q3s**; a mode is never called a failure on a judge verdict alone. Novelty is worth a 3 (Matt).
- ⚠ **NOTHING SURVIVES THE MERGE THAT SAYS WHICH ARM BOUGHT A ROW** — merge between arms, sheets before merge; the pool-view door (GALLERY.md) reads a leg's seat yield after the fact by merge stamp. `PLAN_HEADROOM` 1.6 means a production leg ALWAYS truncates. A scratch driver calling `depth.run` needs a `__main__` guard on Windows.
