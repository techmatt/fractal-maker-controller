## Framing refinement (BUILT ckpt 76, ON by default; mechanics → `curation/framing.py`)
- The location head picks FRAMING within one location on the seating statistic; **adopt only on a strict LOG-ODDS margin (Δ = 2.0 nats, Matt by eye) — a probability margin adopts nothing where `P(≥4)` pins near 1.** Gate-refused framings are never adoptable. The margin STAYS AT 2.0; a scan row at another margin is REFUSED rather than reinterpreted, and anything hunted at 2.0 stays valid if it later moves.
- **★ ONE HOME: HARVEST. Framing happens FIRST, eventually BY THE RUNS, with locations born already-framed (Matt) — which DISSOLVES the migration rather than scheduling it.** No migration to plan, no re-embed to schedule, no viewport question to rule.
- ⚠ **Both smoke-test claims from the ckpt-76 build were WRONG at n=445 — never re-quote either** (the walk-vs-pass "site asymmetry", dead and settled; "sidecar scores within 0.029", an n=12 read). Every refusal is `below_margin`, so the adoption rate reads on FRAMINGS, not coverage. `walk.refine_framings` has never fired — no walk has run since it landed; untested in production, not suspect.

## Novelty & run design (RULED ckpt 62–63)
- **★ THE NOVELTY DIRECTIVE (Matt): "a 1% success rate in a novel region is better than the 1000th beautiful image in Seahorse valley."** Four levers, all BUILT (→ `supply/README.md`): per-lineage supply caps · saturation discount · a protected exploration share · seat-to-budget. All are PACING, never rejection.
- **★ THE SHARE IS A SWITCH, NOT A PRICE:** never-walked lineages book far fewer admissions than the contest (the heads' novelty blindness), so the FLOOR is the whole policy (0.25 ratified) and **the binding constraint is novel-lineage STOCK, not the fraction.** The share buys ground the heads score as junk, by design. Julia is CLOSED to share-novelty — its lever is c-spacing REFILL. Lineage-level identity does not capture gallery-level novelty.
- **Saturation identity = LINEAGE/root-neighborhood FIRST** (palette never counts — geometry is the identity); descriptor clusters are the reserved fallback (parked).
- **★ THE LAST GATE IS THE SOLVE (Matt; BUILT ckpt 84):** a run only feeds the pool; diversity, colour and mode are decided ONCE as constraints on the SET → `preserve\selection_design.md`. **BREADTH BUYS CONSTRAINT SATISFACTION, DEPTH BUYS QUALITY** — a small hunt into the unopened pool closed a colour shortage and did not move a small gallery by a digit. **Mining, not optimizing, is what raises the floor.** The diversity radius is PARTITION-BLIND and the only near-duplicate pairs found were cross-partition.
- **★ THE TWIN GRAPH DENSIFIES AS THE POOL GROWS.** Over one doubling of places, both the share sitting in a twin pair and mean degree among the touched rose. The necessary UPPER bound on non-twin capacity moves freely while the CONSTRUCTIVE lower bound barely does — most of the widening is slack in the bound, not proven capacity. Each new place is likelier to be somebody's twin than the last, and **a thousand-seat gallery is still unproven from below.**

## Mining economics — laws only (every rate → `curation/README.md`)
- **★ EVERY "PRIMED AT k" COUNT IS A MAXIMUM OVER k NOISY SCORES AND IS OPTIMISTIC.** The shrinkage multiplier is k-dependent, bites from about k≥20, is the PRIMED bar's, and **never applies to a per-candidate rate.** Quote a prime count as RAW or CALIBRATED, never mixed.
- **★ PALETTES ARE EXCHANGEABLE WITHIN THE UNRESTRICTED POOL** to at least k=40, and on weak modes to k=96 — clear rate flat in k, no ordering effect, so the palette head's rank buys nothing at the boundary and k is bounded by cost alone. ⚠ **A CARRIER-RESTRICTED SUBSET IS NOT EXCHANGEABLE WITH IT.** **k buys nothing per render and DEEP is much cheaper per render** because the field dump amortises, so **high k strictly dominates for seat-hunting and is exactly wrong when a one-per-location sheet is the product** — the "locations, not candidates, are the scarce thing" condition.
- **★ CONDITIONING MOVES THE COLOUR COLUMN AND COSTS THE QUALITY ONE**, and the composed price still favours it several-fold on a smooth-heavy roster. **CONDITIONING IS MODE-DEPENDENT BY DESIGN (Matt), not defective** — it delivers where the COLORMAP makes the picture's colour and not where a TRAP does; for many strange modes colour targeting is not expected to work at all.
- **★ IT IS THE MAP SET, NOT THE COLOUR.** The carrier set's own *off-target* rows clear far below the general pool's. **Coming out teal costs nothing; being drawn from the carrier set costs everything** — carriers are worse maps whatever colour they land on, and the damage falls on modes with a real `P(≥4)` bar while `P(≥3)`-fallback modes are flat or better. They are not making unusable pictures; they are making pictures the FOURTH cutpoint declines. A themed collection is affordable at reachable scale, bought by paying for a much worse score distribution and taking its thin top — draw biased, render, read the dominance the row already carries, keep what came out dominant; the carrier table is a prior, not a gate.
- **⚠ THE LEDGER-WIDE CLEAR RATE IS NOT A DRAW RATE** — it is dominated by near-band and mode-floor draws on already-proven places; a fresh breadth draw on never-opened locations is what a new arm is read against. **⚠ And those rates are ROSTER FIGURES, not constants** — on a weak-mode roster the conditioned-vs-control sign FLIPS. **On modes that are weak everywhere, breadth buys nothing.**
- **★ DEPTH SUBSTITUTES FOR RANK BUT DOES NOT REPLACE IT.** The head's rank spreads the prime rate several-fold end to end — draw the top half, not the whole range. **CHOOSING THE MODE ROSTER IS WORTH MORE THAN RANK**, and the per-mode field-dump cost spreads across the roster, which is the arithmetic that decision is made on. ⚠ **The spread is a property of the ROSTER DRAWN, not a constant** — read it off the run whose roster you are pricing, never from a doc.
- **★ A BEST-AVAILABLE PERCENTILE IS NOT A MINING READOUT** — it is measured against a growing clearing pool, so every row rises including modes nothing aimed at. **Only `best_rank` separates a real move from the denominator.**
- **★ MINING BUYS SEATS, NOT CEILINGS.** Tens of thousands of renders over four legs moved one mode's `best_rank` by a rounding error while the gallery's bottom quartile and minimum both rose materially and a sixth of the seats turned over. At n=150 the tail is what binds, so that is the right purchase. ⚠ "Aim at MODES, not colour cells" was a one-hour read: **the cells half stands, the modes half does not.**
- **★ THE NEAR BAND IS ~20× BREADTH IN SEATS PER WALL HOUR**, most of its locations priming — but it **cannot be aimed at a mode** (`plan_held_mode` holds the INCUMBENT's) and it is **CONSUMABLE**. ⚠ `--modes` gates near-band eligibility, so `--near-width` is not the only way to spend there. ⚠ `--top-bands` does NOT cut the ranked draw — **`--band-weights` with the lower bands at 0 is the flag.**
- **MERGE BETWEEN LEGS, NEVER AFTER BOTH** — a second leg planned against the pre-merge pool re-draws places the first already took. `curate depth` runs on the locked three workers **partitioned by LOCATION** so the field dump amortises; per-candidate partitioning would re-dump the same field per worker and come out slower than serial.
- **★ "CLEARED" MEANS A `P(≥4) ≥ 0.50` CROSSING AND SAYS NOTHING ABOUT HUMAN q3s.** No instrument looks below that bar, so a mode's q3 supply is UNMEASURED and a mode is never called a failure on a judge verdict alone (Matt). Novelty is worth a 3 (Matt).
- **⚠ THE FLAT-VS-RANKED COST GAP IS REAL, LOCALIZED, AND TWO DIFFERENT NUMBERS** — mean and median differ by half again on the same population and it nearly vanishes read band-against-band on a larger one. Mode mix explains none of it and maxiter none: **every engine stage is dearer and every Python stage is flat**, so it is realized iteration per pixel — the head's rank correlates with locations cheap to iterate. Quote which statistic, or the figure means nothing.
