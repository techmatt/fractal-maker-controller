- **★ AT n=2000 THE CONSTRAINTS CHOOSE THE GALLERY, NOT THE JUDGE.** The judge has no order inside its top (→ fractal-corpus); a judge flip turned over three quarters of the seats and Matt read both as fine. **Retrains buy FLOOR; the top end is Matt's eye on the final seating.** At n=2000 the binding rule is `location`/`twin`, not colour (2026-09-18).
- **★ A MODE FLOOR IS A MANDATE, AND THE MANDATE IS A BETTER CHAIN SEED THAN THE RANKED WALK (2026-09-18).** So the floor is NOT monotone: lowering it to clear a tier-2 shortfall COSTS tier-1 seats (magenta n=400: floor 0 → 396 seats, flat 4 → 399, `seat_floors(n)` → 400). A rising shortfall under a fuller gallery is the lexicographic objective working, not a regression. Every FAMILY pass floors at `mode_policy.seat_floors(n)` since 2026-09-17; a mode pass floors at 0 (→ `GALLERY.md`). A mode pass's residual shortfall is colour `cell_floor` demand, not mode floors.

- **★ A NEW MAP'S SEAT USUALLY TAKES A PLACE THE GALLERY WAS NOT USING, NOT ANOTHER MAP'S SEAT (ckpt 109).** Of 54 new-map seats in a fresh n=1000, **33 sat at places the baseline gallery did not hold, 17 were already seats, and 4 took the place off another row**; across seven records there were five same-place swaps in total, and distinct maps seated ROSE 528 → 542.
