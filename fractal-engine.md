# fractal-engine — render truths that outlive any renderer

The live engine is fractal-wallpapers' `fractal-engine` crate; **its canon is `engine/README.md`** (per-family behaviour, symmetry, `fractional_multibrot`'s branch cut and bailout, mirror-fold, the specialization/perf story, the wasm build and exports, palette stop counts and the bake) and the crate's own docs (`mode.rs` for the modulate mechanics; the rule that the base is spent BY RANK and the degeneracy site live there). fractal-maker is a closed archive; porting is OVER (Matt). Anchors ship as data in fractal-wallpapers. Every ★ below is verified enforced; the guard is the memory — grep the cited test, don't re-argue.

## Rules a future session must not get wrong
- **★ NEVER GATE ON CLIP SHARE — use IN-MASK CHROMA.** Nothing in `src/` gates on clip share. Why it is worthless, and the in-mask chroma table that replaced it → `engine/README.md §The direct traps`.
- **★ UF shape names do not transfer by name**; per-shape thresholds are coverage-anchored.
- **★ EVERY RENDERER DRAWS THE SAME PICTURE FOR THE SAME RECIPE — AND FOUR DID NOT.** The class of bug is a renderer building its engine spec by naming some members and silently dropping the rest. `mine.make` never passed `mode_params`, so every varied candidate in the pool was drawn BARE under its own varied key — 10,664 rows across 13 legs, the route being `depth.Shot`, live until ckpt 117. `candidate_ledger.rerender` had it twice: `render_pair` named four members of `recipes.KEYED`, and `re_render`'s key guard rebuilt each key with `mode_params` pinned to `{}` so every varied row was refused — protective by accident. **The guard is `test_the_two_makers_draw_the_same_picture_for_one_recipe`**: one recipe through every renderer, compared as BYTES, plus a must-differ-from-bare arm so settings that move no pixels cannot pass it. **CLOSED at ckpt 118, and the class was WIDER than three**: `checks`, `run` and `votes` carried `solve`'s omission too, so six renderers were rebuilt behind one `release.task_for` with **no defaults**. The guards that missed it counted named members per builder and stayed green through all four `curve`/`palette` drops — which is the argument for a builder that cannot omit rather than a longer list of keywords. Owned by `curation/README.md §The fix was one builder, not four more keywords`.
- **★ ANY NEW FIELD-SIDE RENDER AXIS MUST ENTER THE FIELD-CACHE/REPLAY IDENTITY KEY** [`test_renders.py`; digests pinned in `test_curation_colorize.py`]. A variant through `mode_params` needs no new axis. **Serialization is a RECORD KEY**: new fields `skip_serializing_if` their default or every cached picture renames. ⚠ **The old claim that colormap-TAIL operators identify via provenance stamps is FALSE in this tree** — `band_autolevel/v1` is a `pixels()` member and identifies THROUGH the identity digest; two bands give two recipe keys. The provenance-stamp route is `ENGINE_FIELD`, engine builds only, sixteen hex characters, and never a gate.
- **★ `KEYED` DID NOT KEY, AND NOW DOES.** A member listed in `KEYED` but absent from what `pixels()` emitted left `key_of` byte-identical, and `pixels()` refused an *unclassified* member while never checking that a *classified* one reached the digest. Closed by `_KEYED_THROUGH`, a declared route per member checked on every call, with a parametrized guard that each member moves the key. Two members need a stated exception and it is the engine's rule not ours: `curve` never reaches the spec for a direct trap, and `mode_params` is only ever non-empty on one mode.
- **★ ⚠ `renders.job_name` IS NOT A DURABLE IDENTITY** — a truncated sha256 of the whole spec INCLUDING `colormap_dir`, an absolute path into the CHECKOUT: a re-clone misses the render cache, a hot-root move does not. **Never pin its digest in a test, and never patch `renders.colormap_dir` to hold a field-name digest** (unenforced; both have bitten). It does NOT carry the mode catalog.
- **★ LEVELLING IS DECIDE-ONCE, REPLAY-UPWARD (ckpt 117), AND NOTHING ENTERED IDENTITY.** The curve is derived at candidate geometry; higher-resolution renders inherit it through `stops_from_stamp` rather than measuring their own. `key_of` is byte-identical over 20,000 live rows. `maybe_level` takes a curve and skips derivation when handed one. **`measured` still means THIS render's own base statistics** — it is the base-render fingerprint and stays honest; a separate provenance field names where the curve came from, and the invariant "the curve follows from `measured`" is conditional on the curve having been derived by that render. `levelling_of` needs no fourth word: a borrowed-curve row has `acted` true and a `curve` present and reads REPLAYED, so the three words the website's `builder/picks.py` owns are untouched. `checks.replay` inherits rather than re-decides. ⚠ The release comparison was taken at 1280×720 ss2, not release geometry; inheriting changed the curve on half the sampled seats and the act-or-not verdict on none of them.
- **★ `autolevel` IS A PIXEL-DECIDING INPUT OUTSIDE EVERY OTHER DIGEST** [`autolevel.applies_to`; the band sha in `recipes.KEYED`; `test_candidate_ledger.py`]. Supersampling and JPEG average colour AFTER the lookup; exact only at ss1/PNG. **The operator's input is the ENCODED picture and the engine route is CLOSED (measured)**. `stamp_of` keeps `{operator, switch, band_sha256}` and drops the curve, so a bare ledger row cannot replay its levelled colormap — but it CAN re-derive, at roughly 0.4 engine-seconds for smooth and 2.0 for stripe, and re-derivation is faithful (1,049 comparable, 1,049 agree, 0 differ). ⚠ **`LEGS.md`'s old note that re-measuring derives a DIFFERENT curve was wrong and is corrected**: the quantity deciding the ramp is the projected statistic, and raw readings outside the band all project to the same edge. The note only holds where a re-measurement crosses a band edge. **`mine.make` records the whole stamp** since 2026-09-08 and the leg writes `sequence.jsonl`; the backfill left **0 outstanding** store-wide, against 6,466 replayable and 623 with no operator at all (the direct-trap family, which has nothing to recover). Owned by `curation/README.md §Levelling is decided once and replayed upward`. **★ ⚠ A `.leveled/` DIRECTORY IS SWEPT ONLY WITH ITS OWN PICTURE, AND A PRUNE IS STRUCTURALLY SAFE** — the whole argument, the replay three-way and the routed-mode trap → `preserve\leveled_identity.md`, corrected 2026-09-09 (the ckpt-111 "NOT SWEEPABLE" wording and a second stale line claiming a re-measurement derives a different curve were both fixed).
- **★ THE FIELD IS DUMPABLE AND A RECOLOUR IS BYTE-IDENTICAL TO A RENDER** [`test_curation_colorize.py`, slow]. **The dump amortises per (location, MODE) — cutting the mode roster is a bigger lever than cutting palettes.** ⚠ **The old 4.8–6.7× figure is not what a rotation pass measures**: with the levelling operator paid either way it is 2.579 engine-seconds a row for six candidates against 0.516 a rotation, and the dump only pays when many palettes share one field — a leg drawing one palette per (location, mode) shares nothing and pays full price. ⚠ **`colorize.render` still refuses a CURVE override together with a `fields` directory**, and correctly: a dumped field is named for its curve and a recolour under an override would be the plain picture wearing the override's name. The PALETTE half of that refusal was wrong and is **closed** — `recolored` takes a `palette`, defaulting to the plain pass of its `mirror`, held byte-for-byte against the render path. ⚠ A field dumped BY MODE NAME carries the CATALOGUE's curve. Composites, direct traps and the angle modes cannot dump.
- **★ THE ENGINE BUILD IS THE FIFTH PIN** (beside colormap, cyclicity, node width, cap policy): `identity.enforce` + `drawn_by.jsonl`; an unstamped or mismatched view is STALE = re-rendered, never scored [`test_engine_fingerprint.py`]. ⚠ `identity.enforce` NEVER SEES A LEDGER ROW — on the candidate side the stamp is provenance and no row is stale, re-scored or refused for it. Scores overlay `score_amendments.jsonl` via `intake.read_scores` — **never open the sidecar directly**. The fingerprint is SPLIT: `IDENTITY_PROBES` · `TRAP_PROBES` · `coverage()` — a NEW mode is invisible to the identity digest (`mode_policy.check` is what a new mode trips), and **the only guard on a direct-trap pixel change is `test_a_regenerated_picture_is_the_picture_that_was_judged`**.
- **★ THE HOME VIEW IS A RUST CONSTANT** — `Family::home_view()` → `Extent::frame()`, 9/16; Python holds NO copy, the one door is `engine.home_view(family)` [`test_home_views.py`; the website's `permalink.test.mjs` pins all nine families]. The julia exception is CRATE-INTERNAL. `CLASSIC_PHOENIX_POINT` is the shared spelling of the pinned phoenix plane.
- **★ TWO SHIPPED MODULATES** [`mode.rs` asserts two]; `itinerary` is production, `tail_itinerary` weight 0 by ruling. Where the base is FLAT the shift is the only variation. Catalogue 8 field + 6 composite + 2 modulate + 4 direct = 20, `de` niche, 19 production; standings → `MODE_POLICY` (fractal-tutorial).
- **★ THE PALETTE IS A RENDER AXIS AND `Palette.phase` / `Palette.cycles` ARE ITS KNOBS** — engine members, already `KEYED`, so a variant is a new row with its own key and needs no derived map, no library admission and no carrier row. ⚠ **`mirror` IS A BAKE AND `cycles` IS AN INDEX, AND THEY MULTIPLY**: the fold happens before the OKLab conversion so a folded map is a different 4096-entry table, and `Palette::place` wraps the index into whatever table was baked — mirror on with `cycles = 2` is FOUR traversals of the base ramp. **Every non-cyclic picture this project ships is already a 2× traversal**, so the repetition ladder on a folded map is even-only. ⚠ **`mirror` is DERIVED from the map's `kind` and never drawn** — `hunt.Maker.palette_for` refuses a draw that sets it, and over all 374,309 ledger rows and all 80 gallery records non-cyclic-unmirrored and cyclic-mirrored are both ZERO. **★ THE FOLDED TABLE CLOSES, SO A REPEAT HAS NO SEAM**: `mirror()` writes the opening colour again at position 1.0, and across all 156 sequential maps the folded wrap step is exactly 0.0 in OKLab against an inside step up to 7.0e-3, where the same maps unfolded wrap at a median 0.693. **★ THE AXIS IS A BYTE-FOR-BYTE NO-OP ON THE FOUR DIRECT TRAPS** — no field, nothing to act on.
- **★ A FLAT TEXTURE IS SMOOTH-WITH-RANK (ruling, ckpt 94)** — `texture_flat` per render, bit-for-bit against `smooth` + rank transfer; the ONE routing spelling is `mode_policy.routed_mode` / `hunt.kind_of`. **`data/coloring/texture_flat.jsonl` is a TRACKED register keyed on the engine spec less the recolour half — GEOMETRY IS IN THE KEY.** It SELF-EXTENDS on a miss at `finished.append`. ⚠ The f32 field dump is NOT ground truth for the texture; truth is the engine's span test.
- **★ `direct_trap_multiply` WHITEWASHES BECAUSE `Blend::Multiply` FROM WHITE IS READ THROUGH sRGB** (mechanism, spread, fix prices → `engine/README.md §The direct traps`). NOT autolevel, NOT the map, NOT mirroring. **The fix is a MODE-PARAM VARIANT, never an engine edit.** ★ **Drawn as candidates for the first time at ckpt 117, and the verdict is: it works and it is small.** Near-white falls from 3.8% of the mask to 0.000 at every cell, but in-mask chroma moves only 0.021 → 0.027 against 0.067–0.138 for every other mode's seats. **Losing white is not gaining colour** — pushed harder the multiply goes through DARKNESS, not through colour. The mode is pastel by construction: a multiply from white is a light picture, and the settings move the lace without moving the ground. Bare clears 40/40; `opacity 0.4` 27/40, `threshold 0.2` 23/40, `opacity 0.6` 19/40, `both` 4/40. ★ **Matt retired the `both` corner by eye (ckpt 117), overriding the ckpt-104 rule that a cell leaves only at zero clears.** The other three stay; they are genuinely mixed and one place went `P(≥4)` 0.035 → 0.987 at `opacity 0.4`. ★ **The frame is a parameter SWEEP, not a roster verdict (Matt): exclude always-bad ranges, sweep the rest.** ⚠ The ckpt-116 reading that this mode cannot make a vivid picture was taken on BARE renders and is retired as evidence. The ckpt-104 roster ruling itself STANDS — the 188 variants of `dtm_variants_20260902` were really drawn and judged at LABEL geometry through the sheet, whose renderer passed the settings; what never happened is a variant reaching the POOL. Defaults in `mode.rs`.
- **★ `release.Task` CARRIES `mode_params`, `curve` AND `palette`; `release.parity` rebuilds its arms by `dataclasses.replace`.** Release geometry, the ss/byte curve and per-seat timings → `curation/GALLERY.md §What a kit costs`. Two verdicts survive here: **bytes FALL with ss at equal q**, and **time a render pilot in SEAT ORDER, never by a byte-size spread**.
- Diagnostic heuristics: **production mirrors non-cyclic colormaps** — treat mirroring as a suspect when a trap-like mode underperforms, but it measured BACKWARDS for `direct_trap_multiply`, so check before assuming. **GENERICITY, NOT MULTIPLICITY, IS THE PERF TRAP** and FAMILY spread dwarfs mode spread — live numbers in website `explorer/README.md §Measured`. A label corpus is selected-top and therefore DEEPER than a random draw: the label-migration leg ran at 1.92 renders a second against a pilot's 2.68 for exactly that reason. **`data/palettes` never stores the baked table size** — compare maps by SAMPLING through the bake's Oklab interpolation, unfolded. **★ ADDING MAPS DOES NOT MOVE AN EXISTING BAKE** — a library addition re-keys nothing and renames no cached picture.
