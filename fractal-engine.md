# fractal-engine — render truths that outlive any renderer

The live engine is fractal-wallpapers' `fractal-engine` crate; **its canon is `engine/README.md`** (per-family behaviour, symmetry, `fractional_multibrot`'s branch cut and bailout, mirror-fold, the specialization/perf story, the wasm build and exports, palette stop counts and the bake) and the crate's own docs (`mode.rs` for the modulate mechanics; the rule that the base is spent BY RANK and the degeneracy site live there). fractal-maker is a closed archive; porting is OVER (Matt). Anchors ship as data in fractal-wallpapers. Every ★ below is verified enforced; the guard is the memory — grep the cited test, don't re-argue.

## Rules a future session must not get wrong
- **★ NEVER GATE ON CLIP SHARE — use IN-MASK CHROMA.** Nothing in `src/` gates on clip share. Why it is worthless, and the in-mask chroma table that replaced it → `engine/README.md §The direct traps`.
- **★ UF shape names do not transfer by name**; per-shape thresholds are coverage-anchored.
- **★ EVERY RENDERER DRAWS THE SAME PICTURE FOR THE SAME RECIPE — AND FOUR DID NOT.** The class of bug is a renderer building its engine spec by naming some members and silently dropping the rest. `mine.make` never passed `mode_params`, so every varied candidate in the pool was drawn BARE under its own varied key — 10,664 rows across 13 legs, the route being `depth.Shot`, live until ckpt 117. `candidate_ledger.rerender` had it twice: `render_pair` named four members of `recipes.KEYED`, and `re_render`'s key guard rebuilt each key with `mode_params` pinned to `{}` so every varied row was refused — protective by accident. **The guard is `tests/test_renderer_agreement.py::test_every_renderer_draws_the_same_picture_for_one_recipe`** (renamed 2026-09-08): one recipe through every renderer, compared as BYTES, plus a must-differ-from-bare arm so settings that move no pixels cannot pass it. **★ ⚠ THE REGISTRY WAS BLIND BY CONSTRUCTION AND IS NOW KEYED BY FUNCTION (2026-09-15).** Its completeness sweep greps for callers of `colorize.render`, which the `render --manifest` door never touches — so that door was invisible to it, and `cli.draw_commands` being present under a different entry is what hid it. The table is keyed by FUNCTION, not module: `draw_commands` alone holds three, and a module key is satisfied by the first. **17 of 19 spec sites are hand-built**, and `engine_spec.spec_of` is the builder that cannot omit, because it reads every member with `[]` rather than `.get`. **CLOSED at ckpt 118, and the class was WIDER than three**: `checks`, `run` and `votes` carried `solve`'s omission too, so six renderers were rebuilt behind one `release.task_for` with **no defaults**. The guards that missed it counted named members per builder and stayed green through all four `curve`/`palette` drops — which is the argument for a builder that cannot omit rather than a longer list of keywords. Owned by `curation/README.md §The fix was one builder, not four more keywords`.
- **★ ⚠ A COLORING FIELD SPELLED FLAT ON A MANIFEST ROW IS LEGITIMATE, AND WAS SILENTLY IGNORED FOR 55,350 ROWS.** `locations.record()` read `mode` / `colormap` / `resolution` / `supersample` only from a nested `render` block, so a row spelling them at top level — which is what the labeling row builder emits — drew `smooth` / `twilight_shifted` / 1920x1080 with exit 0. The flat spelling is the fourth legitimate one and is now read; the omission guard moved to the render door, where the hazard is, because `record()` is shared by three callers wanting different things. ⚠ **Only `trap_circle` and `de` can show a curve omission at all** — every other mode has its production curve written over it. The palette panel's three `trap_circle` cells of sixteen were dumped under the catalogue's curve and re-reading moves 3 of 16 seats; **Matt ruled it self-heals on the next build rather than being re-probed**.
- **★ ANY NEW FIELD-SIDE RENDER AXIS MUST ENTER THE FIELD-CACHE/REPLAY IDENTITY KEY** [`test_renders.py`; digests pinned in `test_curation_colorize.py`]. A variant through `mode_params` needs no new axis. **Serialization is a RECORD KEY**: new fields `skip_serializing_if` their default or every cached picture renames. ⚠ **The old claim that colormap-TAIL operators identify via provenance stamps is FALSE in this tree** — `band_autolevel/v1` is a `pixels()` member and identifies THROUGH the identity digest; two bands give two recipe keys. The provenance-stamp route is `ENGINE_FIELD`, engine builds only, sixteen hex characters, and never a gate.
- **★ `KEYED` DID NOT KEY, AND NOW DOES.** A member listed in `KEYED` but absent from what `pixels()` emitted left `key_of` byte-identical, and `pixels()` refused an *unclassified* member while never checking that a *classified* one reached the digest. Closed by `_KEYED_THROUGH`, a declared route per member checked on every call, with a parametrized guard that each member moves the key. Two members need a stated exception and it is the engine's rule not ours: `curve` never reaches the spec for a direct trap, and `mode_params` is only ever non-empty on one mode.
- **★ ⚠ `renders.job_name` IS NOT A DURABLE IDENTITY** — a truncated sha256 of the whole spec INCLUDING `colormap_dir`, an absolute path into the CHECKOUT: a re-clone misses the render cache, a hot-root move does not. **Never pin its digest in a test, and never patch `renders.colormap_dir` to hold a field-name digest** (unenforced; both have bitten). It does NOT carry the mode catalog.
- **★ LEVELLING IS DECIDE-ONCE, REPLAY-UPWARD (ckpt 117), AND NOTHING ENTERED IDENTITY.** The curve is derived at candidate geometry and inherited upward through `stops_from_stamp`; `key_of` is byte-identical over 20,000 live rows. The whole argument — the borrowed curve, `measured` meaning THIS render's own tone, the provenance field, why `levelling_of` needs no fourth word, and the three words `builder/picks.py` owns — is owned by `curation/README.md §Levelling is decided once and replayed upward`. ⚠ The release comparison was taken at 1280×720 ss2, not release geometry.
- **★ `autolevel` IS A PIXEL-DECIDING INPUT OUTSIDE EVERY OTHER DIGEST** [`autolevel.applies_to`; the band sha in `recipes.KEYED`; `test_candidate_ledger.py`]. Supersampling and JPEG average colour AFTER the lookup; exact only at ss1/PNG. **The operator's input is the ENCODED picture and the engine route is CLOSED (measured)**. `stamp_of` keeps `{operator, switch, band_sha256}` and drops the curve, so a bare ledger row cannot replay its levelled colormap — but it CAN re-derive, at roughly 0.4 engine-seconds for smooth and 2.0 for stripe, and re-derivation is faithful (1,049 comparable, 1,049 agree, 0 differ). ⚠ **`LEGS.md`'s old note that re-measuring derives a DIFFERENT curve was wrong and is corrected**: the quantity deciding the ramp is the projected statistic, and raw readings outside the band all project to the same edge. The note only holds where a re-measurement crosses a band edge. **`mine.make` records the whole stamp** since 2026-09-08, **and both rotation arms since 2026-09-16** (`rotation` in `SEQUENCE_STORES`); every kept seat store-wide resolves a curve (`autolevel survey --record all` is the door; `autolevel backfill --atlas PLANE` reaches a plane's dots). 10,040 non-seat rotation rows still carry none. ⚠ `render --recipe --key` RE-DERIVES the curve at candidate geometry and never read it off the row — the published record redrew levelled before the rotation fix; only the explorer/atlas replay path needed the stamp. **The replay half of the operator is in the WEBSITE's `explorer/engine-wasm/src/level.rs`, not the engine crate, and since 2026-09-16 so is the derivation half** (`derive` + `shade_level`; pinned bit-exact against 4,411 stored curves; serde_json needed `float_roundtrip` — replayed `level=` values were up to one ulp off before). ⚠ The engine crate's `coloring::percentile` is NEAREST-RANK; the operator uses numpy's linear interpolation; they cannot be swapped. Owned by `curation/README.md §Levelling is decided once and replayed upward`. **★ ⚠ A `.leveled/` DIRECTORY IS SWEPT ONLY WITH ITS OWN PICTURE, AND A PRUNE IS STRUCTURALLY SAFE** — the whole argument, the replay three-way and the routed-mode trap → `preserve\leveled_identity.md`, corrected 2026-09-09 (the ckpt-111 "NOT SWEEPABLE" wording and a second stale line claiming a re-measurement derives a different curve were both fixed).
- **★ THE FIELD IS DUMPABLE AND A RECOLOUR IS BYTE-IDENTICAL TO A RENDER** [`test_curation_colorize.py`, slow]. **The dump amortises per (location, MODE) — cutting the mode roster is a bigger lever than cutting palettes.** ⚠ **The old 4.8–6.7× figure is not what a rotation pass measures**: with the levelling operator paid either way it is 2.579 engine-seconds a row for six candidates against 0.516 a rotation, and the dump only pays when many palettes share one field — a leg drawing one palette per (location, mode) shares nothing and pays full price. ⚠ **`colorize.render` still refuses a CURVE override together with a `fields` directory**, and correctly: a dumped field is named for its curve and a recolour under an override would be the plain picture wearing the override's name. The PALETTE half of that refusal was wrong and is **closed** — `recolored` takes a `palette`, defaulting to the plain pass of its `mirror`, held byte-for-byte against the render path. ⚠ A field dumped BY MODE NAME carries the CATALOGUE's curve. Composites, direct traps and the angle modes cannot dump.
- **★ THE ENGINE BUILD IS THE FIFTH PIN** (beside colormap, cyclicity, node width, cap policy): `identity.enforce` + `drawn_by.jsonl`; an unstamped or mismatched view is STALE = re-rendered, never scored [`test_engine_fingerprint.py`]. A rebuild from the same source reproduces the fingerprint on another machine (2026-09-16). ⚠ What a MISMATCHED build actually loses is the score-amendment overlay (`amend.read` keeps only rows at the running fingerprint), not the hunt frames; `curate redraw` re-derives. ⚠ `identity.enforce` NEVER SEES A LEDGER ROW — on the candidate side the stamp is provenance and no row is stale, re-scored or refused for it. Scores overlay `score_amendments.jsonl` via `intake.read_scores` — **never open the sidecar directly**. The fingerprint is SPLIT: `IDENTITY_PROBES` · `TRAP_PROBES` · `coverage()` — a NEW mode is invisible to the identity digest (`mode_policy.check` is what a new mode trips), and **the only guard on a direct-trap pixel change is `test_a_regenerated_picture_is_the_picture_that_was_judged`**.
- **★ THE HOME VIEW IS A RUST CONSTANT** — `Family::home_view()` → `Extent::frame()`, 9/16; Python holds NO copy, the one door is `engine.home_view(family)` [`test_home_views.py`; the website's `permalink.test.mjs` pins all eleven families]. The julia exception is CRATE-INTERNAL. `CLASSIC_PHOENIX_POINT` is the shared spelling of the pinned phoenix plane. **`Multibrot { degree }` is ONE family, degrees 3–6 (`check_degree`)**; degree 6 was added at ckpt 128 with its own written-out specialisation and a `measured_extent` box from a 4001² measurement (home (−0.09, 0), width 4.3) — the wasm carries it (+29 KB raw). Adding a degree is one engine cap, one box, and one-line lists in both repos (`PARAMETER_PLANES`, `degree_of`, atlas `PLANES`, `data/supply/` rows; website `builder/atlas.py`, `permalink.js`/`.test.mjs` `HOMES`, `render.js::familySpecOf`, `builder/explorer.py::CONSTANT_FAMILIES`); the heads see only pixels.
- **★ TWO SHIPPED MODULATES** [`mode.rs` asserts two]; `itinerary` is production, `tail_itinerary` weight 0 by ruling. Where the base is FLAT the shift is the only variation. Catalogue 8 field + 6 composite + 2 modulate + 4 direct = 20, `de` niche, 19 production; standings → `MODE_POLICY` (fractal-tutorial).
- **★ THE PALETTE IS A RENDER AXIS AND `Palette.phase` / `Palette.cycles` ARE ITS KNOBS** — engine members, already `KEYED`, so a variant is a new row with its own key and needs no derived map, no library admission and no carrier row. ⚠ **`mirror` IS A BAKE AND `cycles` IS AN INDEX, AND THEY MULTIPLY**: the fold happens before the OKLab conversion so a folded map is a different 4096-entry table, and `Palette::place` wraps the index into whatever table was baked — mirror on with `cycles = 2` is FOUR traversals of the base ramp. **Every non-cyclic picture this project ships is already a 2× traversal**, so the repetition ladder on a folded map is even-only. ⚠ **`mirror` is DERIVED from the map's `kind` and never drawn** — `hunt.Maker.palette_for` refuses a draw that sets it, and over all 374,309 ledger rows and all 80 gallery records non-cyclic-unmirrored and cyclic-mirrored are both ZERO. **★ THE FOLDED TABLE CLOSES, SO A REPEAT HAS NO SEAM**: `mirror()` writes the opening colour again at position 1.0, and across all 156 sequential maps the folded wrap step is exactly 0.0 in OKLab against an inside step up to 7.0e-3, where the same maps unfolded wrap at a median 0.693. **★ THE AXIS IS A BYTE-FOR-BYTE NO-OP ON THE FOUR DIRECT TRAPS** — `coloring::shade`'s `Direct` arm never receives the `Palette` at all, so the no-op is exact rather than small, and it is the control any phase measurement must pass. **★ AND NOTHING ELSE IS FLAT (2026-09-13)**: 0 of 180 non-trap cells byte-identical, smallest mean Oklab ΔE 0.056, 98–99% of the frame past a JND in all fifteen modes, and the ranking of the fifteen does not separate. ⚠ **The spread inside a mode is the MAP'S KIND, not the mode** — folded 0.305 against cyclic 0.148 — and ⚠ **the dose is not monotone**: a folded map is baked out-and-back, so a half-turn lands on the ramp's reflection and is the largest change there, while a cyclic map's half-turn returns toward its start and reads under half a quarter-turn. Table → `curation/MEASUREMENTS.md`.
- **★ A FLAT TEXTURE IS SMOOTH-WITH-RANK (ruling, ckpt 94)** — `texture_flat` per render, bit-for-bit against `smooth` + rank transfer; the ONE routing spelling is `mode_policy.routed_mode` / `hunt.kind_of`. **`data/coloring/texture_flat.jsonl` is a TRACKED register keyed on the engine spec less the recolour half — GEOMETRY IS IN THE KEY.** It SELF-EXTENDS on a miss at `finished.append`. ⚠ The f32 field dump is NOT ground truth for the texture; truth is the engine's span test.
- **★ `direct_trap_multiply` WHITEWASHES BECAUSE `Blend::Multiply` FROM WHITE IS READ THROUGH sRGB.** NOT autolevel, NOT the map, NOT mirroring. **The fix is a MODE-PARAM VARIANT, never an engine edit.** Mechanism, spread, fix prices, the near-white and in-mask-chroma tables and the clearing counts → `engine/README.md §The direct traps`. **The mode is pastel by construction** — pushed harder the multiply goes through DARKNESS, not through colour, so losing white is not gaining colour. ★ **Matt retired the `both` corner by eye (ckpt 117)**, overriding the ckpt-104 rule that a cell leaves only at zero clears; the other four stay. ★ **The frame is a parameter SWEEP, not a roster verdict (Matt): exclude always-bad ranges, sweep the rest** — and the sweep ran on 2026-09-12 and bought nothing outside the shipped cells (→ fractal-state, `curation/LEGS.md`). Defaults in `mode.rs`.
- **★ THE DIRECT TRAPS' `threshold` IS AN ABSOLUTE DISTANCE IN THE ITERATE PLANE, THE MASK IS "ANY ITERATE WITHIN IT", EVERY HIT COMPOSITES, AND HIT COUNT GROWS WITH `maxiter` — NOTHING NORMALISES IT (audited 2026-09-16).** Screen goes black where no orbit passes the cross (a place property; only `threshold`, the expensive axis, widens it) and white where interior pixels saturate (up to `maxiter` hits); multiply is the mirror, white on a sparse mask. Opacity is the cheap axis and cannot fix an empty mask. **And `weight` in the angle modes is an AMPLITUDE mix toward the screened texture, never a frequency** — the texture's busyness is unnormalised and the interior shows it at full strength whatever `weight` is. The pipeline never varied `weight` (every seat is 0.85); the explorer derives both per view (→ fractal-state §WEBSITE).
- **★ `release.Task` CARRIES `mode_params`, `curve` AND `palette`; `release.parity` rebuilds its arms by `dataclasses.replace`.** Release geometry, the ss/byte curve and per-seat timings → `curation/GALLERY.md §What a kit costs`. Two verdicts survive here: **bytes FALL with ss at equal q**, and **time a render pilot in SEAT ORDER, never by a byte-size spread**.
- Diagnostic heuristics: **production mirrors non-cyclic colormaps** — treat mirroring as a suspect when a trap-like mode underperforms, but it measured BACKWARDS for `direct_trap_multiply`, so check before assuming. **GENERICITY, NOT MULTIPLICITY, IS THE PERF TRAP** and FAMILY spread dwarfs mode spread — live numbers in website `explorer/README.md §Measured`. A label corpus is selected-top and therefore DEEPER than a random draw: the label-migration leg ran at 1.92 renders a second against a pilot's 2.68 for exactly that reason. **`data/palettes` never stores the baked table size** — compare maps by SAMPLING through the bake's Oklab interpolation, unfolded. **★ ADDING MAPS DOES NOT MOVE AN EXISTING BAKE** — a library addition re-keys nothing and renames no cached picture.

## Deep render — SHELVED (Matt, long-term) — THE SINGLE HOME for deep/depth
DEPTH (the f64 pipeline, built ckpt 62, never adopted) and DEEP (perturbation/BigFloat, unbuilt) — vocabulary → settled_rulings; no deep-walk training or labeling. Everything an un-shelving needs is in `preserve\deep_shelf.md`, plus `preserve\sourcing_measurements.md`, `deep/README.md`, and the guards in tree (`test_deep.py`, `test_deep_breadth.py`).

**★ PRECISION, AUDITED 2026-09-17 (`audit_seahorse_precision_minibrot_ckpt129`).** The engine iterates in `f64` everywhere (`Viewport` carries `Complex<f64>`, `engine/src/viewport.rs:19-27`; the website's wasm is the same crate, IEEE-754 with no excess precision) and there is NO deeper regime, not even dormant — the only perturbation is a `TODO(perf)` at `iterate.rs:497-499`; `mpmath` at 60 dps exists solely to say WHERE a nucleus is (`discovery/nucleus.py`), never to iterate a pixel. **The floor is RELATIVE, not a width: `RESOLUTION_ULPS = 4.0`** (`viewport.rs:147`) — a view draws when one SAMPLE step spans ≥ 4 ulp at the coordinates' own magnitude, refused at `spec.rs:321` / `tiles.rs:854`, so supersampling runs out first; in the seahorse valley (|c| ≈ 0.757) that is 4.4e-13 at 1000 px ss1 and **4.5e-12 at release geometry** (2560×1440 ss4). Failure is clean-then-refused, never gradual: 1e-12 draws a sharp minibrot, 1e-13 is refused. The policy floors above it — `walk.Gates.min_width` 1e-9 (a dataclass field), `deep.depth.MIN_WIDTH` 1e-11 (d5/d6 1e-10), the reframing operators' 1.21e-10, `nucleus.SPACING_FLOOR` 1e-13 (retired, still what `Atom.f64_margin` measures against) — are conservative against the live rule; `depth.deepest_releasable_width` is the honest gate. Above the refusal sits a fidelity wall the engine cannot detect (a ~1e-12 picture is deterministic and partly fictional in its finest texture — `deep/README.md`'s contract). Coordinates are decimal strings end to end, but the shallow path writes `repr(float)` (≤17 digits); only the nucleus path carries 35. **The iteration cap is width-scaled and reads nothing else: `maxiter::for_width` = `clamp(4000·(1 + 0.30·log₂(3/width)), 200, 67000)`** (`maxiter.rs:36`; an explicit spec `maxiter` overrides); above it a sample is painted interior (`f64::NAN` → the set's black), so the cap decides what IS interior and two caps are two pictures. **Stripe spends an `atan2` and a `sin` on every iteration (`iterate.rs:607-611`) — ~12.5× smooth at identical cap**; an interior-heavy view is dear for that reason, not depth (`x=-0.7 y=0.2 w=1` ran at cap 5901, the second-lowest on the ladder). Cooperative cancellation inside the iterate loop (a flag the engine checks) is the right final shape for the explorer's cancel latency and is unscheduled.
