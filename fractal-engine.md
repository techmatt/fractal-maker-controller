## Rules a future session must not get wrong
- **★ NEVER GATE ON CLIP SHARE — use IN-MASK CHROMA.** Nothing in `src/` gates on clip share (grepped 2026-09-07). Why it is worthless, and the in-mask chroma table that replaced it → `engine/README.md §The direct traps`.
- **★ UF shape names do not transfer by name**; per-shape thresholds are coverage-anchored.
- **★ ANY NEW FIELD-SIDE RENDER AXIS MUST ENTER THE FIELD-CACHE/REPLAY IDENTITY KEY** [`test_renders.py`; digests pinned in `test_curation_colorize.py`]. A variant through `mode_params` needs no new axis. **Serialization is a RECORD KEY**: new fields `skip_serializing_if` their default or every cached picture renames; colormap-TAIL operators identify via provenance stamps.
- **★ ⚠ `renders.job_name` IS NOT A DURABLE IDENTITY** — a truncated sha256 of the whole spec INCLUDING `colormap_dir`, an absolute path into the CHECKOUT (not the artifact root): a re-clone misses the render cache, a hot-root move does not. **Never pin its digest in a test, and never patch `renders.colormap_dir` to hold a field-name digest** (unenforced; both have bitten; also stated where a test author will meet it, `src/fractal_wallpapers/README.md §Which engine build drew a picture`). It does NOT carry the mode catalog — adding a catalog entry renames nothing.
- **★ `autolevel` IS A PIXEL-DECIDING INPUT OUTSIDE EVERY OTHER DIGEST** [`autolevel.applies_to`; the band sha in `recipes.KEYED`; `test_candidate_ledger.py`]. Supersampling and JPEG average colour AFTER the lookup; exact only at ss1/PNG. **A ledger row cannot replay its levelled colormap** (`recipes.stamp_of` drops the curve; `<key>.leveled/` is RE-RENDERABLE, a `manufacture` row replayable). **The operator's input is the ENCODED picture and the engine route is CLOSED (measured)** — the same field to `.jpg` and `.png` changes the levelled stop list. Since ckpt 103 `sequence.jsonl` carries the whole stamp and `depth.levelling_of` is the three-way reader [`tests/test_depth_stamp_replay.py`]; **★ ⚠ A CANDIDATE'S `.leveled/` DIRECTORY IS NOT SWEEPABLE (ckpt 111), BUT A PRUNE IS STRUCTURALLY SAFE** — `curation/pool_draw.py` resolves one beside a ledger row's own picture and puts it on every plan unit, so a sheet rebuilt without it renders a different picture under the same identity, silently. A ckpt-111 prompt tried to delete all 94,485 and was refused on this, and the standalone sweep is still unsafe for the reason the pinning does not cover: that path has no row in the transaction. The whole argument — the reachable set, the 1.3% of bytes that rules out a partial sweep, why `_delete_colormap` and `pool_draw.leveled_dir` cannot collide, and both halves of `tests/test_leveled_identity.py` — lives in `curation/README.md §A prune cannot take a surviving row's colormap, and the reason is structural`, with a pointer at `LEGS.md`. pre-stamp rows stay UNREPLAYABLE (`acted_unrecoverable`; the website ships the stored file, never a mis-draw; populations → `curation/GALLERY.md §curate solve record`) — re-measuring derives a DIFFERENT curve, so it is not a way out. ⚠ A reader that takes `gallery.mode` (the ROUTED mode) instead of `recipe["mode"]` reads a re-render as "stale"; at the recipe's mode every such seat is sha256-identical to the shipped file (`coloring/README.md §"Bit for bit" is measured`). The `mine` leg's stamp gap is parked.
- **★ THE FIELD IS DUMPABLE AND A RECOLOUR IS BYTE-IDENTICAL TO A RENDER** [`test_curation_colorize.py`, slow; the composite/direct-trap refusal in `mode.rs`]. **The dump amortises per (location, MODE) — cutting the mode roster is a bigger lever than cutting palettes.** ⚠ A field dumped BY MODE NAME carries the CATALOGUE's curve (silent wrongness, not an error). Composites, direct traps and the angle modes cannot dump.
- **★ THE ENGINE BUILD IS THE FIFTH PIN** (beside colormap, cyclicity, node width, cap policy): `identity.enforce` + `drawn_by.jsonl`; an unstamped or mismatched view is STALE = re-rendered, never scored [`test_engine_fingerprint.py`; the gate in `curation/intake.py`]. The stamp is BESIDE the picture. ⚠ `identity.enforce` NEVER SEES A LEDGER ROW — it guards a walk's gate render (floor palette, cyclic map, node width); on the candidate side the stamp is provenance and no row is stale, re-scored or refused for it. The distribution is two-valued and agrees: 173,421 unstamped, 111,096 all on the live build, NOT ONE ROW DISAGREEING. Scores overlay `score_amendments.jsonl` via `intake.read_scores` — **never open the sidecar directly** (unenforced). The pin guards VIEWS only; pre-ckpt-103 candidate rows carry no stamp and are ACCEPTED as unknown-engine (Matt; counts → `curation/README.md §The candidate ledger`); since ckpt 103 every row is stamped as PROVENANCE, never a gate; a mismatch on a candidate never voids the pool; re-render policy per engine change is Matt's. The fingerprint is SPLIT [`engine_fingerprint`]: `IDENTITY_PROBES` (the only digest that stamps) · `TRAP_PROBES` · `coverage()` (stamps nothing) — a NEW mode is invisible to the identity digest (`mode_policy.check` is what a new mode trips), and **the only guard on a direct-trap pixel change is `test_a_regenerated_picture_is_the_picture_that_was_judged`**.
- **★ THE HOME VIEW IS A RUST CONSTANT** — `Family::home_view()` → `Extent::frame()`, 9/16; Python holds NO copy, the one door is `engine.home_view(family)` [`test_home_views.py`; the website's `permalink.test.mjs` pins all nine families as text]. The julia exception is CRATE-INTERNAL. `CLASSIC_PHOENIX_POINT` is the shared spelling of the pinned phoenix plane (`pools.classic_phoenix_pool()`).
- **★ TWO SHIPPED MODULATES** [`mode.rs` asserts two]; `itinerary` is production, `tail_itinerary` weight 0 by ruling. Where the base is FLAT the shift is the only variation — the texture fills the BORING regions. Catalogue 8 field + 6 composite + 2 modulate + 4 direct = 20, `de` niche, 19 production [the split test in `mode.rs`]; standings → `MODE_POLICY` (fractal-tutorial).
- **★ A FLAT TEXTURE IS SMOOTH-WITH-RANK (ruling, ckpt 94)** — `texture_flat` per render, bit-for-bit against `smooth` + rank transfer [the engine guard; `tests/test_texture_flat.py`; the measured pixel proof in `coloring/README.md`]; the ONE routing spelling is `mode_policy.routed_mode` / `hunt.kind_of`. **`data/coloring/texture_flat.jsonl` is a TRACKED register keyed on the engine spec less the recolour half — GEOMETRY IS IN THE KEY.** It SELF-EXTENDS on a miss at `finished.append` (span test at the row's geometry; refuses on a failed probe; readers never render) [`code: labeling/finished.py`]; store membership stays the kind for every reader (→ fractal-corpus). ⚠ The f32 field dump is NOT ground truth for the texture; truth is the engine's span test.
- **★ `direct_trap_multiply` WHITEWASHES BECAUSE `Blend::Multiply` FROM WHITE IS READ THROUGH sRGB** — perceptually it can barely leave white (mechanism, spread, fix prices → `engine/README.md §The direct traps`). NOT autolevel, NOT the map, NOT mirroring (folding REDUCES it). **The fix is a MODE-PARAM VARIANT, never an engine edit** — a settings cell is a new recipe key, nothing re-keys, no label is voided; the shipped white rows stay beside the tinted ones. Adjudicated ckpt 103 (→ fractal-corpus); roster ruled ckpt 104 (five cells; a cell leaves only at zero clears) and confirmed head-to-head on one population 2026-09-05 (`curation/LEGS.md §A roster entry is a mode…`). - **★ `release.Task` CARRIES `mode_params`; `release.parity` rebuilds its arms by `dataclasses.replace`** (fixed 2026-09-04/05, the five builders guarded both ways). Release geometry, the ss/byte curve and per-seat timings → `curation/GALLERY.md §What a kit costs`. Two verdicts survive here: **bytes FALL with ss at equal q**, and **time a render pilot in SEAT ORDER, never by a byte-size spread** (it samples shallow places).
- Diagnostic heuristics: **production mirrors non-cyclic colormaps** — a field piling up at one end loses nearly everything under a mirrored map; treat mirroring as a suspect when a trap-like mode underperforms — measured BACKWARDS for `direct_trap_multiply`, so check before assuming. **GENERICITY, NOT MULTIPLICITY, IS THE PERF TRAP** and FAMILY spread dwarfs mode spread — live numbers in website `explorer/README.md §Measured`. **`data/palettes` never stores the baked table size** — compare maps by SAMPLING through the bake's Oklab interpolation, unfolded (`palettes/README.md`). **★ ADDING MAPS DOES NOT MOVE AN EXISTING BAKE** — measured across all 901 at the ckpt-109 drop; a library addition re-keys nothing and renames no cached picture.
