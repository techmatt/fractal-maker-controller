## EXTRACTION & METHOD CAUTIONS (for every future prompt)
- ★★ Committed CORN score files hold CONDITIONAL sigmoids — unconditional P(≥k) is the running product (full rule → fractal-models). Product-read both sides of any comparison.
- ★★ A blind sheet's base rate is NOT the training base rate (blind_minibrot 3.95× enriched at ≥4) — a calibration arm targets the head's own corpus. **Pre-registered bars get a self-test (the bar must pass the head it was built from) AND must OUT-RESOLVE their instrument** — a floor inside the sheet's margin returns a FAIL the sheet cannot confirm; a control that is the teacher's SELF-AGREEMENT is a ceiling reference, never a bar.
- ★★ The export join must carry EVERY image-determining axis; verify by picture count; `renders verify` (regenerate vs judged, re-compression yardstick) catches what row counts cannot.
- ★★ Select training epochs on a PROPER SCORING RULE (validation loss), never a rank statistic — AP rated a BN-collapsed checkpoint above the sound one; report the whole band.
- ★★ **Never construct a test's expected value with the transform under test** — the big repo's bake-fold guards folded their own references and thereby encoded the bug as the expectation; expectations come from an independent route, and a planted red proves the guard bites.
- ★ **A palette/stop operator is exercised against explicit-end-stop maps before it acts in production; the fold that matters is the LAST one before the LUT** (an upstream fix is a no-op when the bake refolds).
- ★ **Constant homes decide dependency direction:** a light path (stdlib CLI) never imports a heavy module for one constant — move the constant, reverse the arrow, and guard with the import-blocked-subprocess + anti-vacuity pattern.
- ★ **`blue_orange` cross-repo note:** every big-repo render of it (both languages) baked through the wrap fold (max Δ 0.095 linear over 1.59% of LUT entries; → fractal-engine). Matt's eye is calibrated on the FOLDED ramp; this repo renders the same map fold-free — a slight systematic difference on the judging map.
