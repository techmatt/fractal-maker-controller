## Selection — the solve (greedy + swap, ckpt 96)
**The exact solver is RETIRED (Matt, ckpt 96). `curate solve run` is the ONE selection leg at every N** — stratified view → greedy seed → 1-swap improvement to exhaustion, an anytime method — and `curate seat`, `seating.py` and the ILP are gone. **Everything about the leg — the rulings, the measurements, the closed and parked speedups, the census rebase — is `preserve\solver_design.md`; read it before touching `view.py`, `rules.py` or `solve.py`.** What that file and `curation/README.md` do not decide:
- **★ THREE PHASES, STRICTLY ORDERED (Matt):** the location hunt, the wallpaper hunt, then the solve. A run only builds the POOL; **the LAST GATE IS THE SOLVE** (→ fractal-discovery).
- **The recipe key derives THROUGH `renders.spec_of` minus `output` and `colormap_dir`, plus the autolevel band's sha256**; `palette_group` is carried, never keyed. **Scores sit in a sidecar keyed (recipe key, judge artifact, regime), never in the recipe's identity** [ENFORCED: `test_candidate_ledger.py`]. **★ A JUDGE FLIP EMPTIES THE POOL** — `scores_by_recipe` reads live-artifact rows only; adoption + floor refit + FULL rescore are ONE act (checklist → `preserve\judge_training.md`).
- **★ ONE SPELLING PER RULE, ONE OBJECTIVE, ONE `Demand` TYPE** — the seed and the swap loop consult the same predicates; a rule with two copies is the failure the ckpt-96 inventory found (two copies of most rules, two DIFFERENT rules for the group cap). **The tier order is filled → floor shortfall → worst seated → sum; every prune's soundness is a theorem about that order.** Mode floors are the default (`mode_policy.seat_floors(n)`; `--flat-floor` the way off; the record names the rule); a target is `ceil(t × seats filled)`, a share of the realized count, never of n.
- **★ THE VIEW MAY NARROW ALTERNATES, NEVER REACH** — every place's best row is in unconditionally; the replay against the retired greedy is what caught the first view being worse. `headroom.census` (schema 4) bounds the floors; **a schema-3 census is NOT a comparable reading** (it priced the group cap at a rule the leg does not run).
- ⚠ **`headroom._row` and `depth.mode_bars`' `ledger_clear_rate` are CONTAMINATED by aimed rows** (no `drawn_for` filter) — unenforced; the how-to-read is in each site's docstring. The fix is the READER's. ⚠ **The census under-prices non-shareable modes ~4×** (it costs wins off the ledger-wide median render; `smooth_mean_angle` renders at 5.5 s against 0.9 s) — a reader caveat, not a record fix (ckpt 96).
- **Diversity is two rules, both in the leg now:** neutral pre-selection distinctness in the view, and the pixel-cloud twin test at `TAU` as ONE set-level predicate in `rules.py`. **The diversity rule is one replaceable component** — a themed leg swaps in geometry-only distinctness (→ solver_design §Themed).
- **★ LOCATIONS ARE CUMULATIVE, CANDIDATES ARE PER-PASS (Matt):** a pass selects over the whole accumulated pool but seats ONLY candidates it made itself — a location is discovered ground, a candidate is a recolour.
- **The pre-solver gallery pass is DELETED (ckpt 89); the SOLVE and the RELEASE RENDER stay.** The intent worth rebuilding → `preserve\selection_design.md`.

## The colour ceiling
Constraint form, the dominance feature, `carriers.co_dominance` and every constant → `preserve\selection_design.md` and `curation/README.md`. What they do not decide:
- **★ THE CEILING + TARGETS ARE SET CONSTRAINTS IN THE SOLVE** — the per-cell allowance is `floor(K·n/48)+1` (7 at n=150, 9 at n=200); `--target <cell>=t` lifts a cell's allowance and is a `Demand`. **★ AT n=150 THE CEILING BINDS BECAUSE THE CAP ACTS, NOT BECAUSE COLOUR IS SHORT** (ckpt 96): every cell holds ≥37 clearing locations, the under-allowance cells are the objective *declining* colour, and a 1,034-candidate breadth leg moved zero seats. - ⚠ **A single-cell (themed) pool is a NEAR-DUPLICATE pool under the colour-cloud twin metric by construction** — colour comes from the map. Ruled: geometry-only diversity for themed (→ solver_design).
- **The palette group cap is `max(1, floor(0.025·n))`** (Matt) [ENFORCED: `ceiling.group_cap`; `rules.py`]. **The cap is a ceiling against repetition and nothing more — not the quality lever**; there is NO coverage requirement on palettes; the ILP's same-group DISTANCE rule was dropped, not merged (ckpt 96). ⚠ At n=20 the cap is still 1, so any before/after must run at n=150.

## The mining loop
Plan shape, arms, retention, conditioning and every flag → `curation/README.md`. Decisions and traps:
- **★ THE PRODUCTION MINING LOOP IS `curate depth run`, NOT `curate hunt run`** — `hunt` has made well under 1% of the ledger and the shared-field machinery buys it nothing. `depth` fixes its plan before the first render: **nothing terminates adaptively and no stage reads a score** [ENFORCED for the plan shape: `test_depth.py`]. ⚠ A worker's row must carry the parent's key contract — `depth run` was dead for eight hours after a texture_flat commit, the `KeyError` surfacing only after minutes of rendering and reading as a hang; a boundary test now pins it (ckpt 96). **"Cycle" in the old mining-loop sense names nothing in code.**
- The conditioned colour ask is **draw-biased and verdict-measured — never gated on the carrier table.** ⚠ **Read a conditioned arm at the grade the objective needs**: dead at the q4 crossing, it was the CHEAPEST arm per q3 location in the same leg (→ fractal-discovery). **★ RETENTION DROPS ROWS AND THEIR PICTURES TOGETHER, AND IT RUNS INSIDE `merge`** — top-3 per (location, mode) by the rank key, plus four protections: seated in a live release row · human-`rejected` · label-joined · named by `rank_key`'s tracked population. **A picture is kept if and only if its row is** [ENFORCED both directions]. **The point draw is SEEDED** and recorded when absent; **galleries no longer nest.**
- ⚠ **`merge` upserts and the incoming row WINS** (only a human `rejected` verdict is carried back) — and merging an arm removes its locations from the never-opened pool a later UNCONDITIONED draw is taken from: **a selection effect no `drawn_for` filter reaches.**
- **A GALLERY PASS has NO clock** — it runs to completion; hours are an estimate plus a hard-kill backstop. A pass's normal shape is to be INTERRUPTED — the pass id is launch-locked by an OS hold that dies with the process, never a lock FILE. ⚠ Rejection does NOT propagate along a `source` chain. ⚠ `rejection.retire_repeats` reads repeats PER COLLECTION — the design.
- ⚠ **Price a gallery pass's full-size leg off a GALLERY pass's rows, never a run's** — a pass seats the most unlike locations and those skew deep.
- `supply_scores.jsonl` is the only cheap record of the admitted population — DURABLE; `curate run` refuses on missing/short [ENFORCED: `run.py` → `durability.guard`]. The flatness sidecar is durable too. The signature sidecar (`curate signatures sweep`) is rebuildable, not durable. ⚠ `flat16` is blind to a dead TEXTURE layer — `texture_flat` is the instrument (→ fractal-engine).
