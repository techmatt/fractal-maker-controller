## Selection — propose-then-solve (BUILT ckpt 84)
Modules, commands and every mechanic → `curation/README.md`; the ruled design → `preserve\selection_design.md`. What those two do not decide:
- **★ THREE PHASES, STRICTLY ORDERED (Matt):** the location hunt, the wallpaper hunt, then the solve. A run only builds the POOL; selection is `curate solve` over the candidate ledger, and **the LAST GATE IS THE SOLVE** (→ fractal-discovery).
- **The recipe key derives THROUGH `renders.spec_of` minus `output` and `colormap_dir`, plus the autolevel band's sha256**; `palette_group` is carried, never keyed. **Scores sit in a sidecar keyed (recipe key, judge artifact, regime), never in the recipe's identity** — a judge adoption invalidates scores and nothing else.
- Solver facts that stop a rebuild going wrong: **HiGHS gives a MIP no warm start at all** (measured), so incremental re-solve is a dead end and no second solver dependency was taken; what made it scale is a SOUND lower bound on the pixel-cloud metric plus fixing stage 2's columns against the incumbent floor. ⚠ **A CORRELATED PROXY IS NOT A PRUNE** — location distance failed because colour comes from the map, not the place.
- **★ THE CENSUS–GREEDY GAP IS ZERO AT EVERY SIZE TESTED, including n=150 with the twin rule acting** — exact optimization has nothing to buy below that; the money goes on supply.
- **★ ⚠ THE GREEDY CANNOT FILL A FLOOR ABOVE 1 AND SILENTLY DISAGREES WITH THE ILP** — `seating.seat`'s scarcity leg `break`s on the first successful seat per mode [code: seating.py:632], so floor 1 and floor 2 produce a bit-identical gallery while the exact solver honours the floor [code: solve.py:1061]. Fix = keep seating that mode until the floor is met or its subpool is spent; nothing else reading the leg assumes one-per-mode. State's OPEN 1.
- **★ ⚠ THE ORDER IS `merge` → `curate flatness sweep` → `seat`.** `seating.seat` RAISES `SeatingRefused` before the walk when any clearing candidate has no value for the active key; `--allow-unranked` is the one way past and is for a picture on disk that will not decode. That refusal is the fix: before it, unranked rows sorted last and were never refused, so thousands of merged rows sat in the pool clearing their bars and unable to win a seat, **silently.**
- ⚠ **`headroom._row` computes `renders / supply` over the WHOLE ledger with no `hunt.drawn_for` filter**, so aimed rows raise its numerator far faster than its denominator and it under-prices a targeted win, with no scalar correction; `depth.mode_bars`' `ledger_clear_rate` reads the same contamination.
- **Diversity is TWO rules and neither is in the solver:** neutral pre-selection at pool construction for geometric distinctness, and the pixel-cloud twin test as sequential state in the seating. `solve.RADIUS` is RETIRED — a second spelling of one fact.
- **★ LOCATIONS ARE CUMULATIVE, CANDIDATES ARE PER-PASS (Matt):** a pass selects over the whole accumulated pool but seats ONLY candidates it made itself — a location is discovered ground, a candidate is a recolour.
- **The pre-solver gallery pass is DELETED (ckpt 89); the SOLVE and the RELEASE RENDER stay.** `EXTRA_PICKS` and the intent worth rebuilding → `preserve\selection_design.md`.

## The colour ceiling
Constraint form, the dominance feature, `carriers.co_dominance` and every constant → `preserve\selection_design.md` and `curation/README.md`. What they do not decide:
- **★ THE CEILING + TARGETS ARE SET CONSTRAINTS IN THE SOLVE** — exact count budgets, no ordering, no fallback leg — and **the ceiling is sequential STATE in seating, never a proposal-time filter**, because a strange attempt's family mass drifts from its smooth-field recolour.
- ⚠ **A HARD TARGET AT 1.0 BUYS THE COLOUR BY SPENDING EVERY OTHER AXIS** — feasible and bad at small n, and at moderate n it goes infeasible on the PAIRWISE rows rather than the ceiling: many seats in one cell are near-duplicates of each other, which no hunt relieves. Mine, then solve over a filtered pool.
- **The palette group cap is `max(1, floor(0.025·n))`** (Matt), replacing the identity cap of 1. ⚠ **THAT REASONING IS FALSIFIED (built, then replayed):** relieving the cap barely moves mean prior map tier and buys FEWER distinct maps rather than better ones, realizing well under its allowance. **The cap is a ceiling against repetition and nothing more — not the quality lever.** ⚠ **There is NO coverage requirement on palettes and never will be**; the colour distribution is a separate mechanism over dominant cells read off the FINISHED picture. ⚠ At n=20 the cap is still 1, so any before/after must run at n=150. ⚠ The ceiling's dry replay is a **scratch script, not a command.**

## The mining loop
Plan shape, arms, retention, conditioning and every flag → `curation/README.md`. Decisions and traps:
- **★ THE PRODUCTION MINING LOOP IS `curate depth run`, NOT `curate hunt run`** — `hunt` has made well under 1% of the ledger, and at its shipped defaults draws 3 distinct modes without replacement, so k=1 at every (location, mode) and **the shared-field machinery buys it nothing.** `depth` fixes its plan before the first render: **nothing terminates adaptively and no stage reads a score.** **"Cycle" names nothing in code.**
- The conditioned colour ask is **draw-biased and verdict-measured — never gated on the carrier table.** Retention drops pictures, never rows. **The point draw is SEEDED** and the seed is DRAWN and RECORDED when absent, never defaulted; **galleries no longer nest.**
- ⚠ **`depth` is NOT field-modes-only** despite stamping `field_modes_only` unconditionally — `--modes` is unchecked against `colorize.shareable`, so a composite RUNS there, and the plan cycles UNIFORMLY: mixed in beside field modes, composites take an equal count at much higher unit cost and eat the breadth. **A composite needs its own bounded leg.**
- ⚠ **`merge` upserts and the incoming row WINS** (only a human `rejected` verdict is carried back), so a merge re-attributes recipes that already existed under another run; and merging an arm removes its locations from the never-opened pool a later UNCONDITIONED draw is taken from — **a selection effect no `drawn_for` filter reaches.**
- ⚠ **`--finish-by` does not pace, it is `harvest`'s alone, and `curate depth` does not have it** — depth sizes off a budget in WALL seconds plus a rate measured at ITS width, and refuses without the rate. **A GALLERY PASS has NO clock** — it runs to completion; hours are an estimate plus a hard-kill backstop.
- **A pass's normal shape is to be INTERRUPTED** — the pass id is launch-locked by an OS hold that dies with the process, never a lock FILE. ⚠ Rejection does NOT propagate along a `source` chain. ⚠ `rejection.retire_repeats` reads repeats PER COLLECTION — unscoped it would retire wallpapers that are a run diagnostic and a gallery seat standing on one place, which is the design.
- ⚠ **Price a gallery pass's full-size leg off a GALLERY pass's rows, never a run's** — a pass seats the most unlike locations and those skew deep, with a per-row spread more than an order of magnitude wide.
- `supply_scores.jsonl` is the only cheap record of the admitted population — DURABLE; `curate run` refuses on missing/short.
