## Judge method (governs every retrain)
- **★★ THE WINNER RULE:** candidate only if (a) no overall pre-declared metric significantly worse (95% paired bootstrap) AND (b) the motivating slice significantly better, none worse; declare slices before numbers.
- **★★ NON-INFERIORITY READS THE BAND (Matt-ratified, ckpt 72):** for parity-motivated candidates the bar is "no pre-declared metric significantly worse ON THE BAND"; a per-seed conjunction is a multiplicity trap (5 arms × 3 seeds = 31.6% false FAIL against a perfectly non-inferior candidate) — any future per-seed conjunction must PRE-STATE its false-alarm size at registration. Bought by the joint-judge adoption: the medium backbone's unanimous strange failure was DATA STARVATION, not architecture (the +0.101 shared-vs-split gap was 100% scale / 0% order and vanished at the small backbone).
- **★★ CROSS-SEED COMPARISONS SCORE THE BAND, NEVER THE STAGED PICK ALONE.** A pick that spends a selection set leaves the BAND as the honest read.
- **★★ THE MODEL-SELECTION OBJECTIVE IS A CONTROLLED VARIABLE** — freeze selection to the baseline-comparable population; amendment before the re-run.
- **★★ CORN scale is train-prior-calibrated** — a retrain moves the whole probability scale; restatement modes → fractal-operating. Conditional-sigmoid caution → fractal-tutorial §CAUTIONS.
- **A head's render cache must be COMPLETE against its stored rows before it trains** — `renders plan`/`renders build` are the precondition; shortfalls → fractal-tutorial §THIS ERA. The location head's three regimes (640×360 ss2 canonical · 640×360 ss1 · 384×216 ss1) are cached complete and row-comparable under regime-addressed tile identity (record tile_regime_identity). **The REGIME-ROBUST head is ADOPTED (ckpt 59)** — one score SCALE across all three regimes, not one NUMBER (re-scoring 68 rows deploy→node moved mean −0.048, max |Δ| 0.28; never read a cross-regime delta ≤0.3 as a model change); rows carry their regime as PROVENANCE, not semantics (records retrain_location_regime · regime_flip_read · adopt_location_regime). Lesson kept: **a prereg's POPULATION is load-bearing** — the split-based consistency read FAILED on a ceiling (78% agreed-zero eval rows) while the same candidate PASSED cleanly on stratified production stock; measure a decision on the population it serves, and expect train rows to flatter any cross-regime consistency metric.
- **Never finetune.** **★★ Never augment in a way that destroys the evidence the label was based on.**
- **Division of labor:** the location head STEERS within-family and NEVER ranks cross-family · ONE render judge (**`render`, ADOPTED ckpt 72** — small backbone, shared CORN classifier, pooled stores; band verdict: strange parity, smooth ordering equal with far better calibration) scores FINISHED renders of BOTH kinds · the palette head picks palettes within a location, never quality · steer/rank/select stay split. smooth/strange are KINDS: `smooth` = one mode, strange = the other 17 production modes (audit_rendering_modes); `smooth_render`/`strange_render` still name the two LABEL STORES (unmerged: two corpora, two blind instruments, two base rates, two floors) but no model. **A shared nominal scale does NOT license cross-kind ranking** — pools disjoint, floors per kind: the same number can be above one kind's floor and below the other's bar. **Any row with `scores.p_ge4 == null` is on the retired 3-class scale — evidence of nothing current.**
