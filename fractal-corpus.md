## Laws
- **★ A LABEL ROW CARRIES ITS JOIN.** Label + complete render block in the SAME tracked row; labels ship FLAT; eligibility DERIVED, never stored; no path, so no `artifacts/` delete can reach a row [`finished.check` refuses a row with no `render_key`; `test_history_purity.py` bans absolute paths].

Quote the RATIO, never counts. **The rows that DO join are the SELECTED TOP** — a standing caveat on every read off them. **Zero seated pictures carry a label BY RECIPE KEY — a regime artifact, not a seating fact**: labels are 1280×720 ss2, candidates 640×360 ss2, so a recipe-identity join is empty for every mode by construction (ckpt 104); 19.8% of seats sit at a labelled PLACE, four rate there 38% `[human n=678]`. **The selected-top caveat is 59%, not all**: 41% of label rows come from sheets built to escape the top (`manufactured_rare_colors`, `under_seen_modes`, `smooth_decision_bands`, the calibration pair).
- **★ A ROW LANDS IN THE STORE ITS MODE — AS ROUTED — SENDS IT TO** [`finished.routed_to` CALLS `hunt.kind_of`; `finished.routes_to` reads the `texture_flat` register]. **A flat-texture modulate row is SMOOTH (ckpt 94), and STORE MEMBERSHIP IS THE KIND for every reader** — `finished.resolved` never consults the register; only `finished.check` (write path) and `finished_train.population` do. Ckpt 104: all 120 movable flat rows in `strange_render` carry a smooth revision (smooth 6,300); **3 stay flat-and-unrevised at `smooth_render`-pinned places by design** (`sparse_mode_head_top.jsonl` lines 504/505/515) — never move them. A batch revised into a store it was never registered in is registered there with its flags copied verbatim.
- **Export/ingest:** `labels\` is untracked — **THE INGESTED STORE IS THE COMMITTED RECORD; ritual = export → ingest in the SAME session**, count-verified, both stores, pin asserted before and after [`finished.assert_pin_holds`, injected at `labeling/intake.py`]. ## Sampling regime
Split-registry rulings → `settled_rulings.md §Corpus sampling`. What still changes a read:
- **★ The disqualifying bias for an EVAL set is MODEL-DRIVEN SELECTION, not non-randomness.** Nominal splits are not realized splits. **Eligibility derives from THREE fields: `eval_only or (score_unconditioned and not anchored)`** [`registry.py:105`; `test_label_registry.py`, `test_eval_pins.py`]; `eval_only` is a PIN and OUTRANKS. Register a generation method BEFORE its batch is built; caveats → `data/batch_caveats.md`.
- **★ ⚠ REGISTRATION ALONE DOES NOT PIN.** The pin is `eval_split.jsonl`; `label ingest` never refreshes it. `pins.every_pinned` (union over every store) vs `pins.pinned` (location store alone); the reframe channel refuses a pinned place as seed AND frame [`test_reframing_channel.py`]. **Fix the split, never the pin**; `write_pin` TRUNCATES. ⚠ The guards catch CONTRADICTION and OMISSION, not correction.
- **★ A BLIND SHEET INFORMS EXACTLY ONE BOUNDARY**; blind sheets are eval-only forever; a rate off any prefilled sheet is a CEILING. Standing instruments: blind_minibrot (≥4; 197 rows, 186 from ONE maker production-walk ledger; **48.7% q4 is the base rate the reframe channel is read against**) · blind_modes (≥2/≥3 freely; **≥4 has zero blind positives — NEVER read it** [`test_eval_pins.py`]). ⚠ smooth ≥4 order on blind_minibrot is ≈ chance for both render judges; the LOCATION head ranks its own q4 nuclei in-sample (median P(≥4) 0.843). Nothing covers smooth ≥3.
- **★ A BOUNDARY SITTING IS ±k AROUND THE CROSSING, NEVER "TOP k INSIDE A BAND"** — over a 3,018-location band "top 120" landed at 0.941–0.950, and "mode-balanced top nine per mode" put 118 of 120 above 0.5; both were meant as boundaries and neither was (ckpt 103, twice). The phoenix ±100 draw was the shape that worked. **A prefilled-4 page measures DOWNWARD disagreement only** (294 of 300 prefilled 4 → all 162 corrections ran down; fours-from-below untestable).
- **phoenix:classic labels by store (ckpt 103):** the LOCATION store holds 73 rows / 7 q3+ / 1 q4; the FINISHED stores hold strange 103 rows / 30 q4 and smooth 210 rows / 20 q4 on the plane — **its scarcity is PLACES, not verdicts** (q3+ places 38 → 72 this era). An earlier "one q4 label on the plane" read the location store alone.

- **★ THE `P(≥4)` COLUMN IS A WELL-CALIBRATED ≥3 SCREEN WEARING A ≥4 NAME.** No threshold on it buys tier-4 precision; every threshold buys ≥3 purity (phoenix smooth at 0.5: 93 of 95 q3+). **A themed leg reads its yield at `P(≥3) ≥ 0.50`.** ⚠ A map's prior label history splits by store (helps smooth, HURTS strange); the pooled reading is INVALID. **★ `P(≥4) ≥ 0.5` IS TRUSTED AS A QUALITY GATE (Matt, 2026-09-02)** — he raises quality issues himself. **On the phoenix planes 0.5 is not a bar at all**: 39 of 73 fours sat below it, best-separating threshold at chance; the judge over-rates `curvature`/`gaussian_int` there and under-rates the direct traps and `itinerary`.

## Sittings — the correction loop IS the labeling method
Mechanics → `labeling/README.md`; assume proper randomization. Suggestions come from the SHIPPED head via `floors.SCORING_HEAD` — re-check every load site after a judge adoption (done for v6). Sheets are cut ORDERED BY P(≥4), pre-labeled, sorted good→bad (default), ~500 rows a sitting; VERIFY the labels earned their keep. A stated RULE answers a unit BEFORE it renders; rule verdicts DERIVED, never pooled across rule ids. Per-sitting correction rate is the head's report card and a CEILING; on unseen modes agreement is NOISE; quote the hand-cast prefix and note any swept suffix.
- **★ A sheet is ALWAYS a fresh render at `sheets.LABEL_RESOLUTION`** (the single spelling); score every sheet picture at label geometry too and carry BOTH readings (`selected_on` + `columns`, on 888 rows now). Cost tracks GATE STATUS + DEGREE — pilot per-class ON the target population. **★ ⚠ AUTOLEVEL IS INVISIBLE ON THE ROW** — measure a pool sheet at label geometry BEFORE building it; a plan written without its `leveled` key serves correctly and reproduces wrongly.
- **★ ONE POPULATION SPANNING BOTH KINDS IS TWO SHEETS** (`finished.check` routes by `hunt.kind_of`). ⚠ `finished_source` writes `facts`, `columns` and a caption UNCONDITIONALLY — rewrite `sheet.jsonl` with them emptied for a blind sheet. ⚠ `--port` walks upward — read the banner; a labeller server outlives its session (kill it, or it holds the port and a little commit).
- **★ A SHEET SHOWS ONE RESULT PER LOCATION (Matt)** — label-harvest legs want BREADTH at low k; seat legs want DEPTH at high k. **★ WHEN THE OBJECTIVE IS HARVESTING q3/q4 IN SPARSE MODES, LABEL THE BEST ONLY (Matt).** Look-first, label-on-divergence.
- **★ A q3+ VERDICT IN ANY STORE IS A PROVEN ROOT (ruled ckpt 103)** — `supply.proven.derive` unions the location store and both finished stores, credited on `provenance.store` (→ discovery). Every sitting grows the proven channel.
