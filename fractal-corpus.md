## Laws
- **★ A LABEL ROW CARRIES ITS JOIN.** Label + complete render block/params in the SAME tracked row; a label keyed on an id whose meaning lives in an untracked file is orphaned when that file dies (proven three times). Labels ship FLAT — label + join + batch id + registration flags; eligibility DERIVED, never stored per-row; no path, so no `artifacts/` delete can reach a row [ENFORCED: `finished.check:270` refuses a row with no `render_key`; `test_history_purity.py:182` bans absolute paths in tracked records].
- **★ ⚠ THE LABEL STORES AND THE CANDIDATE LEDGER BARELY OVERLAP, BY PLACE AND NOT BY KEY.** Most labeled rows sit at a place that never became a candidate or a family the ledger lacks; the reader-side join ceiling is ~12% and a correctly built geometry-blind key recovers ZERO rows (tombstoned). ⚠ Quote the RATIO, never the counts — both stores grow every sitting. **The rows that DO join are the SELECTED TOP** (tier-4 share nearly triple the rest) — a standing caveat on every read off them.
- **★ A ROW LANDS IN THE STORE ITS MODE — AS ROUTED — SENDS IT TO** [ENFORCED: `finished.routed_to:229` CALLS `hunt.kind_of(mode, texture_flat)` (`hunt.kind_of:853`) rather than copying it; `finished.routes_to(row)` takes the render's identity to the `texture_flat` register; refused at write, excluded at the training read, stored rows untouched]. **A flat-texture modulate row is SMOOTH (ruling, ckpt 94)**: 107 of the 201 resolved itinerary renders re-route to `smooth_render` reader-side, strange training population 4,616 → 4,509; no eval pin was touched. ⚠ **Three re-routed rows stand at places `smooth_render`'s pinned set holds** — costs nothing while they live in strange's files, but a future smooth-store batch re-rendering one of those places would be TRESPASS; check before building one.
- **Export/ingest:** the `labels\` drop is untracked — **THE INGESTED STORE IS THE COMMITTED RECORD; ritual = export → ingest in the SAME session**, count-verified, both stores, pin asserted before and after [ENFORCED: `finished.assert_pin_holds` (`finished.py:531`), injected as the `assert_pin` field at `labeling/intake.py:249,262`]. ## Judge method — reading caveats only (the method itself → `preserve\judge_training.md`)
- **★ THE RENDER JUDGE IS A GATE, NOT A TOP-END RANKER.** Inside `P(≥4) ∈ [0.60, 0.95)` it barely orders Matt's 3-vs-4 verdict, no ≥4 crossover is identifiable in any stratum, a cut there mostly discards Matt's own 4s, and hard optimization of the score there is optimizing noise. Every in-band row came back ≥3, so the ≥3 crossover sits BELOW 0.60, unmeasured. **The ceiling is narrower than that sounds:** unbanded on a grouped holdout the head orders human 3-vs-4 at `AUC(≥4)` 0.650 — what resists is ordering INSIDE the head's own uncertainty band. One resolvable calibration: at matched score a composite is materially likelier to be a 4 than a thin-colour candidate, so **relaxing a bar for a thin cell compounds the error.**
- **★ THE `P(≥4)` COLUMN IS A WELL-CALIBRATED ≥3 SCREEN WEARING A ≥4 NAME.** Its calibration gap is an order of magnitude worse than `P(≥3)`'s; a threshold sweep inside a selected population holds tier-4 precision FLAT across four decades while ≥3 purity climbs to 1.000, and `AUC(≥4)` inside the seats is ≈0.5. **No threshold on this column buys tier-4 precision; every threshold buys ≥3 purity.** **Consequence (ckpt 96): a themed (single-cell) leg reads its yield at `P(≥3) ≥ 0.50`, never at the q4 crossing** — a conditioned arm dead at q4 was the cheapest arm per q3 location in the same leg. ⚠ **A map's prior human label history splits by store — helps smooth, HURTS strange, both CI-excluding; the pooled cross-store reading is INVALID and must never be quoted.** The colormap route is ruled out regardless; a colour DESCRIPTOR off the finished picture stays open.
- **★ THE RENDER JUDGE IS NOT REGIME-ROBUST.** Across one doubling of geometry `P(≥4)` is UNBIASED and IMPRECISE, re-levelling adds to the spread, and nearly half of mid-band rows change a 0.10-wide band. **Fit and read on the LABEL-GEOMETRY column, never the candidate one.** A prereg's POPULATION is load-bearing. The location head is REGIME-ROBUST in the opposite sense: one score SCALE across regimes, not one NUMBER — never read a cross-regime delta ≤0.3 as a model change (→ fractal-discovery §Regime law).
- Committed CORN score files hold UNCONDITIONAL P(≥k); a blind sheet's base rate is NOT the training base rate. Label noise, the winner rule, non-inferiority, restatement, test construction and costs → `preserve\judge_training.md`.
- **Division of labor:** the location head STEERS within-family, NEVER ranks cross-family, and picks FRAMING within one location (→ fractal-discovery) · **ONE render judge (`render`) scores FINISHED renders of both kinds** · the palette head picks palettes within a location, never quality (split keys on LOCATION — settled) · steer/rank/select stay split. **smooth/strange are KINDS**; `smooth_render`/`strange_render` name two LABEL STORES, not a model, and a shared nominal scale does NOT license cross-kind ranking — pools disjoint, floors per kind.
