## Laws
- **★ A LABEL ROW CARRIES ITS JOIN.** Label + complete render block in the SAME tracked row; labels ship FLAT; eligibility DERIVED, never stored; no path, so no `artifacts/` delete can reach a row [`finished.check` refuses a row with no `render_key`; `test_history_purity.py` bans absolute paths].
- **★ ⚠ THE LABEL STORES AND THE CANDIDATE LEDGER BARELY OVERLAP, BY PLACE AND NOT BY KEY** (ratios → `MEASUREMENTS.md §What the label stores and the candidate ledger share`); ⚠ THAT REGIME ENDED AT ckpt 117. `label_migration merge` put 5,329 scored rows (human classes 3 and 4, derived at candidate geometry) into the ledger, 3,015 of them new, so every one of Matt's 2,107 graded-4 wallpapers is a ledger row BY KEY. The derivation is an identity claim, not a resemblance — re-rendered keys came back byte-identical. The old by-place-not-by-key rule is history, not a constraint. Quote the RATIO, never counts. **The rows that DO join are the SELECTED TOP** — a standing caveat on every read off them (59% of label rows; the rest come from sheets built to escape the top). **Zero seated pictures carry a label BY RECIPE KEY — a regime artifact, not a seating fact** (labels 1280×720 ss2, candidates 640×360 ss2).
- **★ A ROW LANDS IN THE STORE ITS MODE — AS ROUTED — SENDS IT TO** [`finished.routed_to` CALLS `hunt.kind_of`; `finished.routes_to` reads the `texture_flat` register]. **A flat-texture modulate row is SMOOTH (ckpt 94), and STORE MEMBERSHIP IS THE KIND for every reader** — `finished.resolved` never consults the register; only `finished.check` (write path) and `finished_train.population` do. The register SELF-EXTENDS at `finished.append`, refusing on a failed probe [`code: labeling/finished.py`]; probe prices → `labeling/README.md`. Three flat rows stay unrevised at `smooth_render`-pinned places by design (`sparse_mode_head_top.jsonl`; `batch_caveats.md`) — never move them. A batch revised into a store it was never registered in is registered there with its flags copied verbatim.
- **Export/ingest:** `labels\` is untracked — **THE INGESTED STORE IS THE COMMITTED RECORD; ritual = export → ingest in the SAME session**, count-verified, both stores, pin asserted before and after [`finished.assert_pin_holds`, injected at `labeling/intake.py`]. Ingest OLDEST DROP FIRST; a drop's name carries the BATCH, not the sheet directory (a re-cut shares its path — delete a superseded cut). Mechanics → `labeling/README.md`.
- **Canonical reader = `store.resolved()` / `finished.resolved()`** [`test_label_store.py`, `test_finished_store.py`] — never write another. Originals never modified; a revision is a later row, LATEST-WINS by `(recorded_at, file, line)`; `labeler` is not in the key. An already-labelled exclusion that leaks lands as a revision and spends nothing.
- **★ THE SCALE IS 1–4 FOR EVERY HEAD; class 4 is just a class.** Anchor sheets DEFINE each bar; store scale is DECOUPLED from model class count [`finished.py`, `finished_train.py`, `finished_import.py`, `finished_acceptance.py`]. Location label = max over crops; `decoded_class` ≠ human label. ⚠ No label row carries a `scores` column — a `scores.p_ge4` filter is a no-op and a candidate-pool fact.
- **★ `labeler:null` = MATT'S LABELS**; Matt is the ONLY human labeler. Label-origin rules protect exactly eval-split integrity and taste attribution; otherwise a label is a label.
- **★ `friend_votes` IS AN ATTRIBUTE STORE (ckpt 107; ingest unbuilt)**: thumbs-up (1) / star (2) from named friends over a full kit, labeler = the name typed, never the 1–4 scale, never pooled with a quality store; nothing trains on it. Its use is Matt's to dictate once the votes are in. ⚠ **The kit is NOT on state's OPEN list and never was** (checked 2026-09-12) — it is unraised rather than active, and Matt raises it himself if he wants it. **★ DELIVERY IS A LARGE ZIP FROM MATT'S DRIVE, NOT A HOSTED PAGE (ruled 2026-09-09)** — friends must see the wallpaper at FULL SIZE, which any lossy multi-tier web kit gives up; do not re-propose hosting. **The deck is randomized per friend**: ONE master permutation, each friend an evenly-spaced ROTATION of it, pages of 25, "go as far as you like but finish any page you start", with page index, position, timestamp and page-completed on every vote row. Viewer 3.0; the seed lives in the kit manifest so a rebuild does not rotate unreached pages out from under anyone. ⚠ A 1,000-picture kit costs **21.2 h of rendering at ss4** (5.4 at ss2) and lands at ~1.15 GB zipped — a leg to plan, not an afterthought.
- **★ `spiral` IS AN ATTRIBUTE STORE, NOT A QUALITY HEAD (ckpt 104)** — a LOCATION property, two classes, never the 1–4 scale, never pooled; pinned in `data/spiral/eval_split.jsonl`. ⚠ An attribute store's `Records.assert_pin` asserts NOTHING at ingest; `attributes.assert_pin_holds` is the real guard. **The sitting SATURATED — another spiral sitting buys nothing; the residual error is the encoder's.** Rates and method → `data/spiral/README.md`, `models/spiral/README.md`.

## Sampling regime
Split-registry rulings → `preserve\rulings_corpus.md §Corpus sampling`. What still changes a read:
- **★ The disqualifying bias for an EVAL set is MODEL-DRIVEN SELECTION, not non-randomness.** Nominal splits are not realized splits. **Eligibility derives from THREE fields: `eval_only or (score_unconditioned and not anchored)`** [`labeling/registry.py`; `test_label_registry.py`, `test_eval_pins.py`]; `eval_only` is a PIN and OUTRANKS. Register a generation method BEFORE its batch is built; caveats → `data/batch_caveats.md`.
- **★ ⚠ REGISTRATION ALONE DOES NOT PIN.** The pin is `eval_split.jsonl`; `label ingest` never refreshes it. `pins.every_pinned` (union over every store) vs `pins.pinned` (location store alone); the reframe channel refuses a pinned place as seed AND frame [`test_reframing_channel.py`]. **Fix the split, never the pin**; `write_pin` TRUNCATES. ⚠ The guards catch CONTRADICTION and OMISSION, not correction.
- **★ A BLIND SHEET INFORMS EXACTLY ONE BOUNDARY**; blind sheets are eval-only forever; a rate off any prefilled sheet is a CEILING. Standing instruments: `blind_minibrot` (≥4; **48.7% q4 is the base rate the reframe channel is read against**; its one-ledger provenance → `labeling/README.md`) · `blind_modes` (≥2/≥3 freely; **≥4 has ZERO BLIND positives — NEVER read it**: the four score-4 rows in the resolved store are a later ANCHORED pass moving rows 3→4 inside the ≥3 class, so the ≥3 positive set is byte-identical before and after [`tests/test_render_head.py::test_blind_modes_is_blind_at_the_boundary_it_gates_on`]). ⚠ smooth ≥4 order on `blind_minibrot` is ≈ chance for both render judges; the LOCATION head ranks its own q4 nuclei in-sample (`models/location/README.md`). Nothing covers smooth ≥3. · **`blind_palettes` (ckpt 109)** — 50 new-drop maps against 50 shipped maps, unconditioned, matched on mode roster and location-score band; the ONE boundary it informs is **≥3, new library vs shipped: 42% vs 32%, ±14pp at fifty a side.** It rules out the drop being worse and resolves nothing finer. It is the project's only rare-colour-adjacent blind instrument.
- **★ A BOUNDARY SITTING IS ±k AROUND THE CROSSING, NEVER "TOP k INSIDE A BAND"** — wrong twice at ckpt 103 (`batch_caveats.md`); the phoenix ±100 draw was the shape that worked. **A prefilled-4 page measures DOWNWARD disagreement only.**
- **★ ⚠ THE SHEET IS A COVARIATE, AND THE DRIFT CAN LIVE INSIDE ONE BATCH (ckpt 119).** Across `p_fine_correction_20260909`'s three cuts, χ² = 69.39 over 750 rows and **79.66 inside the judge-selected top band** (p = 1.4e-13): sheet 1 gave **no 1s at all** to its 100 top-band rows against 32 and 28 on sheets 2 and 3, block means 3.33 / 2.14 / 2.37. It is not composition — sheet 1's rows were marginally the WORSE ones by both the fine column and the render judge. The bottom two blocks show no drift. **Any pooled rate off a multi-sheet sitting can hide a 1.2-tier spread**; `row["sheet"]` carries the cut. **Matt ruled it accepted as label noise** — not modelled, not reweighted, not excluded — but a train/stopping split must STRATIFY on sheet, or a recall gap moves for a reason that is not fit.
- **★ A COARSE 3 GRADES AS A FINE 1 (measured ckpt 119).** 100 unconditioned coarse-3 rows came back **84 × 1, 14 × 2, 2 × 3, no 4s**, mean 1.18, flat across modes (1.00–1.56) and kinds (1.17 / 1.25) — an offset of about two tiers. Nothing else in the tree measures the coarse-to-fine relation. **A retrain mixing a coarse tier into a fine target without that offset is mixing two scales**, and auto-promoting coarse rows into fine labels is REJECTED (Matt, ckpt 119): coarse verdicts are a SAMPLING instrument, and the fine label stays Matt's own.
- **`phoenix:classic`'s label scarcity is PLACES, not verdicts** (per-store counts → `batch_caveats.md §ONE-CARD-A-PLACE`); a read off the location store alone under-counts.

## Judge method — reading caveats only (the method → `preserve\judge_training.md`)
- **v6 is the render judge (ckpt 103; v5 on disk, revert one act; adoption UNCONDITIONAL, by Matt's eye on the acceptance sheets).** `MEASURED_RELEASE_FLOORS` refit — **the new rows moved the crossings by exactly zero; the whole shift is v6's scale**. Pool bar stays the flat 0.50.
- **★ THE RENDER JUDGE IS A GATE, NOT A TOP-END RANKER — AND HAS NO ORDER INSIDE ITS OWN TOP, ON ANY KIND (ckpt 103).** Spearman with Matt's tier ≈ 0 on every top-slice read (`MEASUREMENTS.md`); v6 puts a flat 1.0000 on thousands of rows (`models/render/RUNS.md`). Two thirds of what the judge is surest about is a three. Consequence → discovery §What binds: the constraints choose the gallery; retrains buy FLOOR; **the top end is Matt's eye on the final seating (ruled ckpt 104 — no label-feedback loop, not tracked).** The one place it orders inside its top is the rare-colour slice, where the disagreement is COLOUR-SHAPED: purple and lime at 0.99 to the judge, ~3 to Matt's eye.
- **★ THE `P(≥4)` COLUMN IS A WELL-CALIBRATED ≥3 SCREEN WEARING A ≥4 NAME** — no threshold on it buys tier-4 precision and every threshold buys ≥3 purity; **a themed leg reads its yield at `P(≥3) ≥ 0.50`** [`GALLERY.md §The q4 bar is a statistic on the record`]. **★ `P(≥4) ≥ 0.5` IS TRUSTED AS A QUALITY GATE (Matt, 2026-09-02)** — he raises quality issues himself. **On the phoenix planes 0.5 is not a bar at all** [`data/batch_caveats.md §BAND-DRAW`]; ⚠ **the PER-MODE half of that reading is refused by the same caveat** — the modes stand on disjoint judge-selected places, so a per-mode rate off those labels is not differenceable. **Judge-disliked hues are FIVE, not four** — green, lime, cyan, teal and yellow, 40 of 120 maps, below the other seven at every tier holding the human tier fixed [`data/strange_render/README.md`]; ⚠ the rose comparison this doc used to carry exists NOWHERE in the tree. **★ THE HUE-BIAS WORRY IS CLOSED (Matt, by eye, ckpt 115)** — the fine head scores the light-cool cells honestly, so lime and cyan thinning as the bar rises is a supply fact rather than a calibration artifact. **★ THE SCALAR IS THE BEST LINEAR READ OF ITS OWN LAST LAYER — CLOSED, NEVER RE-RAISE**: a frozen trunk sits at chance on AUC(≥4) while a fully unfrozen one reaches 0.72, so the fine order is not in the shipped representation and must be trained into the trunk [`models/gallery_grade/README.md`]. **★ AND THAT HEAD'S LEVEL IS TRUSTED, NOT ONLY ITS ORDER (Matt, 2026-09-07)** — `p_fine(≥4)` reads as an absolute quality target. ⚠ **This SUPERSEDES the ckpt-113 seating-only line, and the repo marks it superseded** — a later prompt restating the old line is a reader taking a retired position as current, which has already happened once. **★ `rank_key` IS NOT RETIRABLE (ckpt 114, still true at HEAD)** — `cascade_order` is built on `ranking_for`'s mapping, so the cascade IS the rank key below the bar; ckpt 116 removed only the OFFER. ⚠ Its margin over the raw judge is GONE — the numbers, and the judge's own smooth AUC moving as the store grew, are carried by `curation/rank_key.py`'s module docstring and `curation/GALLERY.md`. ⚠ "A map's prior label history splits by store" is asserted nowhere and has no measurement in the tree to check it against — UNVERIFIED, and not to be quoted as a finding. Evidence → `preserve\rulings_corpus.md`.
- **★ THE `direct_trap_multiply` TINT — ADJUDICATED (ckpt 103).** Matt's eye is CHROMA-INDIFFERENT; only the judge penalised the tint; the variants buy fours, not keepers. **RULED ckpt 104: a settings cell leaves the roster only at ZERO clears — different clearing ranges are diversity.** The per-cell label read was ARGMAX-PER-PLACE (`batch_caveats.md`); the matched head-to-head is `night_d` (`LEGS.md §A roster entry is a mode…` — two populations, two numbers, both right).
- **★ THE RENDER JUDGE IS NOT REGIME-ROBUST.** Fit and read on the LABEL-GEOMETRY column, never the candidate one. The location head is REGIME-ROBUST in the opposite sense: one score SCALE, not one NUMBER — never read a cross-regime delta ≤0.3 as a model change.
- **★ THE MEASURED CROSSOVERS AND THE ACTING BAR ARE DIFFERENT NUMBERS.** `MEASURED_RELEASE_FLOORS` are isotonic fits per KIND; only the strange one gates anything, at a run's release. The POOL bar is a flat `0.50` — ruled round (Matt, ckpt 98).
- Committed CORN score files hold UNCONDITIONAL P(≥k). **Division of labor:** location head STEERS within-family, picks FRAMING within one location · ONE render judge scores both kinds on ONE scale — **"within its own kind" is a reading caveat, never a mechanism: the rank compares across kinds** · palette head picks palettes within a location; steer/rank/select stay split. **smooth/strange are KINDS**; a shared nominal scale does NOT license cross-kind ranking; **two judges in one column is two estimands — a flip is always a FULL rescore, never lazy.**

## Sittings — the correction loop IS the labeling method
Mechanics → `labeling/README.md`; assume proper randomization. Suggestions come from the SHIPPED head via `floors.SCORING_HEAD` — re-check every load site after a judge adoption. Sheets are cut ORDERED BY P(≥4), pre-labeled, sorted good→bad (default), ~500 rows a sitting; VERIFY the labels earned their keep. A stated RULE answers a unit BEFORE it renders; rule verdicts DERIVED, never pooled across rule ids. Per-sitting correction rate is the head's report card and a CEILING; on unseen modes agreement is NOISE. **A correction sheet cannot also measure the judge — the BLIND sheet is THE INSTRUMENT** (`finished_import.py`).
- **★ ⚠ THE SCALE MOVES WITHIN A SITTING AND WITHIN A PAGE, AND IT IS THE LARGEST EFFECT IN THIS STORE.** ~0.967 tiers between the first and last cut of one afternoon, and −0.425 tiers per 100 cards within a single page. **Two thirds of the store's 1.7-tier cross-sitting spread is scale rather than pictures**, measured within-row on 116 rows graded twice. ⚠ **`anchored=false` IS NOT A SHARED FOOTING** — the same rows read 1.33 tiers apart across two batches both carrying it. **Never pool grades across batches without saying so; report within sitting.** Full evidence → `preserve\gallery_grade_stability.md`.
- **★ CUT ASSIGNMENT IS RANDOMIZED FROM A RECORDED SEED, AND THE DRAW ITSELF NEEDS CONDITIONING.** Cut must be independent of score, mode, cell, leg and seat — but a realized draw can still come out unbalanced on the prefill (p = 0.046 on `gallery_top_20260910`), so a cut comparison conditions on the prefill and reports the raw means marked as confounded.
- **★ A DELIVERED SHEET MUST NOT BE COMPARED ACROSS ITS OWN SIDES WHEN THEY ARE UNEQUALLY FRESH.** `aug_sweep_A` could never have a fresh-vs-fresh cut: every adopted-only pick was already a seat in the record the earlier sittings graded, so 100 of 100 carried a prior verdict against 16 of 100 on the other side. Design a comparison page so both sides are equally new, or state the asymmetry on the page.
- **★ A sheet is ALWAYS a fresh render at `sheets.LABEL_RESOLUTION`**; score every sheet picture at label geometry too and carry BOTH readings (`selected_on` + `columns`). Cost tracks GATE STATUS + DEGREE — pilot per-class ON the target population. **★ ⚠ AUTOLEVEL IS INVISIBLE ON THE ROW** — measure a pool sheet at label geometry BEFORE building it; a plan written without its `leveled` key serves correctly and reproduces wrongly.
- **★ ONE POPULATION SPANNING BOTH KINDS IS TWO SHEETS** (`finished.check` routes by `hunt.kind_of`). ⚠ `finished_source` writes `facts`, `columns` and a caption UNCONDITIONALLY — empty them for a blind sheet. ⚠ `--port` walks upward — read the banner; a labeller server outlives its session.
- **★ A SHEET PREFILLS *AND ORDERS* ON THE LABEL-GEOMETRY DECODE (ckpt 111)** — `finished_source` renders at `--resolution`/`--supersample` and `score_pictures` scores those files, so both `columns` and the page order come from `1280x720 ss2`; a revision sheet's prefill is the incumbent verdict but its ORDER is still that decode. Every one of the 12,491 stored rows in both finished stores carries that geometry, without exception in 41 batches, and no finished-render row carries `selected_on` at all. **The anchoring confound this creates is REAL and is NOT what produces the label-geometry advantage** — anchored rows show a SMALLER advantage than clean ones. **★ A SHEET SHOWS ONE RESULT PER LOCATION (Matt)** — label-harvest legs want BREADTH at low k; seat legs want DEPTH at high k. **★ WHEN THE OBJECTIVE IS HARVESTING q3/q4 IN SPARSE MODES, LABEL THE BEST ONLY (Matt).** Look-first, label-on-divergence.
- **★ THE JUDGE'S PER-MAP TOP-k IS NOT THAT MAP'S BEST SHOWING (ckpt 109).** Ten maps scored nothing above a 2 across 40 judge-chosen places, and **eight of those ten produced real wallpapers at places chosen for HUMAN q4 evidence** — same maps, same mode, only the chooser of the place differing. **Pick PLACES by human evidence and let the judge rank pictures within a place**; a sheet that asks the judge to choose both the place and the picture cannot support a per-map verdict, and every per-map read off `new_maps_top4_20260905` carries that caveat.
- **★ A q3+ VERDICT IN ANY STORE IS A PROVEN ROOT (ruled ckpt 103)** — `supply.proven.derive` unions the location store and both finished stores (→ discovery). Every sitting grows the proven channel. **★ ⚠ BUT A SITTING ON THE HEAD'S TOP OFFER RETURNS ROOTS WITHOUT PARAMETERS (ckpt 123)**: `julia_supply_ckpt123`'s 48 rows became 48 proven roots standing on **9 distinct `c`, none of them new**. Labelling the top of an offer deepens the twin channel exactly where the labels already are and widens it nowhere.
- **DONE (ckpt 123): `julia_supply_ckpt123`** — 48 rows, two sections, both never opened and carrying no prior verdict: julia side 23/24 at q4, parameter-plane reference side 22/24, both sides q3+ unanimous. ⚠ **Prefilled 4 on all 48**, so the agreement rate is a correction-page ceiling; the sound read is the comparison BETWEEN the sides, which says julia's top end is worth what a well-mined plane's top end is worth. One plane 3 sat at rank 3 of 24.
