- **★★ `loc_id` stability is a silent-failure seam** — the aug cache maps `loc_id` → coordinates; a manifest rebuild that renumbers points every tile at the wrong location and training still completes with plausible numbers. **Any rebuild asserts tile↔location agreement in BOTH directions first.** ⚠ A RE-SPLIT renumbers `loc_id` and invalidates the cache — but a full rebuild is now ~3 h (fractal-models), so this is a cost, no longer a one-way door.

## ★★ The BLIND EVAL instruments — sheets D and E (2026-08-11, permanent)
Anchored correction sheets measure AGREEMENT with the incumbent, not quality; these two are the eval-side answer and they are **eval-only forever** — never train, never re-drawn, never re-spent.
- **Sheet D** `2026-08-11_wallpaper_blind_minibrot_v1`, **197 rows**, tiers **{1:0, 2:6, 3:95, 4:96}** — *no tier-1 label anywhere.* **197 of a 200 target is SUPPLY-BOUND**, so the sheet is the whole eligible population and no selection rule remains that could bias it. ⚠ **It was drawn behind the location head's `GOOD_FLOOR` and the production colorize path, so its 97.0% ≥3 is the base rate of the GATED minibrot intake, not of raw minibrot material** — with 6 negatives, ≥3 is barely measurable and ≥2 is undefined. Its live boundary is **≥4** (48.7% tier-4).
- **Sheet E** `2026-08-11_render_mode_blind_v1`, **150 rows** over 110 locations, tiers **{1:74, 2:70, 3:6}** against the K=3 ceiling. Its live boundary is **≥2**; at ≥3 it has almost no positives. Deliberately absent from `near_dup_groups.BATCHES`, which is the mechanism keeping it out of training.
- **★★ A BLIND SHEET INFORMS EXACTLY ONE BOUNDARY, AND ITS DRAW'S QUALITY CONDITIONING CHOOSES WHICH** — not the labeling. Sheet D has no negatives at ≥2; sheet E has almost no positives at ≥3. Design the conditioning for the boundary you intend to referee, or the instrument answers the wrong question (state §OPEN 2).
- **Blind + eval-only mechanics (both sheets):** `ROW_KEYS` declared and asserted at write time, holding exactly `image_id / sheet_order / render / provenance / label` — **no `suggested_tier`, so the rig cannot enter correction mode**, no head block, no score field. Order is a seeded shuffle stamped in `sheet_order`, served `&order=file`. All rows `split_side=eval`; `batch.json` stamps `eval_only`. A tokenizing SOURCE SCAN fails each builder on any head symbol, and that scan has its own guard proving it catches a real emission while ignoring prose describing one.
- **★★ THE EVAL-ONLY PIN — a THIRD split constraint** [code: `tools/corpus/eval_only.py`]. Protocol §2a's two fixes (freeze one batch set as authority / re-derive globally) both choose an authority for a CONTESTED location and neither has anything to say about a slice that may never train at all — so the pin OUTRANKS both and is wired into every split pass that could see one. Applied at **UNIT granularity** (pinning one member and drawing the rest is exactly the straddle `split_units` exists to prevent) and **inert when empty — the rng is consumed identically, so the live render-mode split is byte-identical**. `mining_corpus.load_corpus` now reports **110 forced keys, 0 present in the current pool — a MEASURED 0, not an absent check.** The wallpaper trainers assert on the **c-INCLUSIVE coordinate, never the `image_id`**, so a future batch re-rendering a sheet-D location under a fresh id cannot spend the instrument.
- **★★ §2a — CONTRADICTING STAMPED SPLITS FORCE A GLOBAL RE-DERIVATION.** The three mining batches disagreed: sheet B's 91 locations are a subset of the v1 sitting's 112 and **33 are stamped train by one, eval by the other**; sheet C is stamped 100% train. ## ★ Sitting design — standing rules

## The labeling rig — reuse, don't rebuild
`tools/viz/corpus_label.html?batch=…` (+ `?manifest=` for a blinded manifest), served by `tools/viz/serve.py`, which resolves relocated crops transparently.
- **★★ CORRECTION SHEETS are the sitting default (Matt, 2026-08-05/06):** the relevant head pre-labels (suggestion-cut method → fractal-models), presentation **SORTED good→bad by the head's score** (not shuffled), suggestion prefilled, positional accept-all-below sweep behind confirm; unreviewed suggestions never leave the page as labels. No blind rows, no calibration aids (unchanged, firm). The blind/shuffled rig below remains for adjudication and non-correction sittings. **★ A BUCKETED CUT DOES NOT ALSO RESERVE** [code: `cut_sitting`; found 2026-08-07]: the calibration reservation is a fill-path mechanism, so every bucketed sheet skips `phoenix:classic` by construction (still 7 positives) — it needs its own explicit bucket or a non-bucketed sitting. **"Ranked residue" means tier ≥ 2 everywhere** — tier-1 rows carry no canonical decode: nothing to correct, nothing to sort by. Correction sheets serve off registered batches via the rig, never `build_combined_label_sheet` (its every property is the negation — it strips scores and deals seeded apportionment precisely so ranking cannot anchor; guard checks both paths).
- **Seeded shuffle per batch — never draw order.** Blind by default: hides score/verdict/arm, degree/period/band, `image_id` (encodes fate), prior labels. `r` reveals; reveal state recorded and exported [0 reveals across ~1800 labels].
- **A SUBSET of a built sitting re-serves as a filtered blind manifest** (same registered batches, no new registration, no re-render); select by stamped status (`derive_t_good.adopted_statuses()`), never a hand list [built + verified 2026-08-05].
- Canonical label-crop + VIVID `blue_orange` companion together — canonical is what the model sees, vivid is what Matt judges from. Export = ONE `labels.json`; revisions → `merge_amendments.py --apply`; fresh rows → `merge_scores.py --max-score 4`. **⚠ Opaque-keyed sheet exports are INVISIBLE to `test_label_reachability`** — a never-merged export reads identically to a merged one; verify merges by row count, never by the guard (gap open).
- **Anchor first, bulk second** for a fresh scale — but an anchor batch is CURATED: it calibrates a bar, it does not estimate a population. Calibration aids: never (fractal-operating, firm).
- **★ The batch-design template (amended 2026-08-03, Matt):** ONE combined sitting ≤1000 rows, single manifest + single export (`?manifest=…&order=file` — the page reshuffles client-side otherwise), **apportionment-sequenced via `tools/apportion.py` across (source_batch × family)** — the ±1 prefix bound is POPULATION-DEPENDENT (~1.5 at 13 skewed cells; structural only at 2 cells): ASSERTED on the built order, never assumed [measured 2026-08-05]. ## Label noise — what it forbids
A ceiling effect localized to the old top category — resolved by the class-3 revisit; disagreement is adjacent-category only: a real ordinal scale with a fuzzy boundary, not a learnability ceiling. **Forbids: reading small AUC differences on the ≥3 boundary.** Pre-register bars before computing; verify the instrument's inputs actually change (`measurement_practice.md`). **Matt: "noise is expected at all boundaries" — do not re-raise.**
